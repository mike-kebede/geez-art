# Deep-dive craft specs — Ethiopian Classical Design Language

Generated by the 15-agent fan-out. These are the executable specifications for motion, illumination, color, type, and data design.

## Stage 1 — deep dives


### Manuscript illumination & frame geometry — the frame system, lattice/interlace, corner motif, and their motion

**Verdict:** The current execution (taste-test-news-article.html) is geometry-blind decoration, and each primitive fails for a concrete, fixable reason. (1) The lattice band (lines 145-148): a 6px-tall stripe built from two 45°/-45° repeating-linear-gradients at a 7px period, 50% opacity. The gold "strap" is ~7px thick on a 6px band, so strap ≈ repeat ≈ band height: there is no air, no over/under, no continuity — it renders as a herringbone hash that on a dark panel goes muddy-gray and moirés while scrolling. A 7px period is the single worst scale for rasterization (1px and 6px both alias into a smear). It is crossed lines, not interlace. (2) The corner diamonds (lines 150-152): a flat 9px square rotated 45°, with a non-integer 1.5px border (aliases on retina — never use 1.5px) and a background fill that punches a notch through the frame. Four per frame, PLUS the same diamond reused as a spinning "gem" in every lattice divider (line 179): the diamond is multiplied into a motif, which violates "one memorable thing" and reads as pure decoration. (3) frameIn (line 153) is translateY(12px)+fade — a generic card entrance, not the signature "manuscript frame inscribing on load"; the defining motion of the identity is not implemented at all. (4) The loader (lines 159-171): three 6px triangles orbiting a 12px circle at 2.6s with an opacity dip — that is a stock CSS spinner; there is no weave, no assembly into a woven diamond, nothing that feels inevitable. (5) There is no system: one border weight everywhere, no grade/density ladder, no light-vs-dark material behavior, no scale-collapse rules — the same fat frame appears on a 220px card and a full-width hero, which is exactly why it reads as "horrendous." All of this is conceptual in DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md §5 (described as "gold interlace band" and "corner diamonds") but never given geometry.

**Spec:**

## System promise
The frame is a PROPORTION SYSTEM, not an ornament. It exists on a strict module M = 4px; the only non-module dimension in the whole system is the 1px stroke (the scribe's hairline, deliberately the one exception). The frame has an anatomy that is always the same five parts, and only the middle part may ever carry pattern: outer rule → air → ORNAMENT ZONE → inner rule → content gutter. Air and gutter do more work than any strap. Decoration is HELD AWAY from content by air — that is the manuscript craft and the whole source of subtlety.

## Frame anatomy — the two-rule manuscript stack (outside → inside)
Every framed container uses this order:
1. Outer hairline — 1px, 40% of its full color. It "holds the wall."
2. Air gap (the manuscript "waste") — 8px at Grade 1, 4px at Grade 2, 6px at Grade 3.
3. Ornament zone — the weave band; width = density tier × 8px (8/16/24). Bare (empty) at Grade 1.
4. Inner hairline — 1px at 70-80% of its full color. This is THE line; it owns the content. (On parchment: umber ink #15090B at 0.7. On umber-black panels: parchment #F8E6B8 at 0.5 or pale gold #E5C193 at 0.6.)
5. Content gutter — the clearance from inner rule to text: cards 12px, articles 16px, tables 8px, modals 24px, empty-states 20px.

## Frame grades (rule+band cost; gutter separate)
- Grade 0 — bare hairline: 1px only. For tables, form fields, plain article body, modal chrome. Cost 1px. This is the calm default; most of the app lives here.
- Grade 1 — the scribe's double rule: outer 1px@0.4 + 8px air + inner 1px@0.7. Cost 10px. Corner = stitched corner (below). Default for cards.
- Grade 2 — the weave frame: outer 1px@0.4 + 4px air + 16px weave band (2x tile) + 4px air + inner 1px@0.7. Cost 26px. Corner = rivet lozenge. For LIVE rate panels, the featured article, terminal views.
- Grade 3 — the illuminated frame: outer 1px@0.5 + 6px air + 1px flank + 24px band (3x tile) + 1px flank + 6px air + inner 1px@0.8. Cost ~40px. For the boot screen and the hero headpiece ONLY — the gallery's single largest painting.

Ratio cap: rule+band ≤ 1/14 of the container's short side. Enforced by the collapse ladder (below) — never by eye.

## Corner motif — the rivet lozenge (replaces the flat diamond)
Geometry: a lozenge (elongated rhombus) whose LONG axis points along the frame diagonal toward the corner. Long axis 16px, short axis 8px (2:1 — a deliberate echo of the 3:2 manuscript page folded). 1px outline, NO fill. Centered 12px in from the corner of the inner rule, so it floats in the air, not on the line. At its center, a 2px dot. The lozenge is a jeweler's setting; the dot is the jewel.
- Grade 2: single 1px outline + 2px dot.
- Grade 3: double outline (inner outline offset 1px) + 3px dot.
- Grade 1: NO lozenge — the stitched corner: the outer and inner rules stop 4px short of the corner and a 1px diagonal stitch of 12px (at 45°, bisecting the corner) closes the gap. One stitch, nothing more.
- Grade 0: nothing.
State (this is how the corner connects to the identity): dot = gold when live (#C9962E on light, #E5C193 on dark), lifted red #E8836F when alert, and on loading the dot pulses 0.45↔1 opacity over 900ms cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.6,1). The corner rivet is the eye-dot's seat: the frame's live-ness is signaled by the corner, never by adding more gold.

## Lattice / interlace — exact tile geometry
A weave is OVER/UNDER, not crossed lines. This is the entire fix.
Tile T = 16px (4M). Two diagonal strap families at +45° and −45°, spacing between parallel straps 8px (T/2) — a 2×2 diamond grid per tile. Strap weight w = 1px. Over/under: at each crossing the NE–SW family passes over where (family index + strap index) is even — a deterministic checkerboard. Rendered in two layers: (1) the full recess grid in the recess color; (2) the gold over-straps, each interrupted by a 2px gap centered on its under-crossings (1px each side), so the gold visibly passes OVER the recess strap. This over/under is what makes it read as woven at every zoom, and it works at 1px because the two tones (gold over, recess under) carry the depth that the old 50%-opacity herringbone tried to fake.
Density tiers (band height = tile height; 1x crops to half a tile):
- 1x "air": T=16, straps 1px, spacing 8px, SINGLE layer (no gaps — the whisper is a pure engraved hatch), deep gold #C9962E at 0.32 on parchment / pale gold #E5C193 at 0.22 on black. Band 8px. Reads at arm's length as "the hint of a weave" — invisible until you look.
- 2x "weave": T=16, straps 1px, spacing 8px, full two-layer over/under. Band 16px. Reads as woven texture; each crossing resolves over/under on close inspection. This is the workhorse.
- 3x "garden": T=12, over-strap 2px (gold) + under-strap 1px (ONE accent color only), spacing 6px, band 24px, flanked by the 1px hairlines. The only tier that admits color into the strapwork, and it admits exactly one accent (madder #A62F1E or verdigris #1E8A5E, never saffron here) at 0.85. Rich illumination, used once.
Reading at 1px vs 6px (the explicit question): at 1px strap with ≥2px air between crossings, the weave reads as fine texture — subordinate, legible. At 6px strap, strap ≈ band height and ≈ repeat, so intersections merge into a solid checkerboard that moirés on scroll and smears to gray at high-DPI. The current 7px-period/6px-band is the worst possible scale. Absolute rule: strap ≤ T/8 and air between strap edges ≥ 2× strap. If a 6px visual band is ever wanted, it is built from THREE 1px straps with 2px air between (a woven ribbon), never from one 6px strap.

## Margins, gutters, gallery hang
Framed containers hug their own edge: outer padding 0; all interior air is the gutter. Unframed (Grade 0) containers use 16px padding. Wall spacing between framed panels (the gallery hang): 16px horizontal, 24px vertical on desktop; 12px/16px on mobile. Frame inset from viewport edge on parchment walls: 16px. On umber-black panels the frame sits 8px from the panel's own edge.

## Scale-collapse ladder (this is what kills "horrendous fat frames on small cards")
Min short side per grade, from the 1/14 ratio: G0 <140px (no frame); G1 140-364px; G2 364-560px; G3 >560px. Below 300px short side, only an L-frame (band on top + one side) at the allowed grade, never a full rectangle. Height ≤48px (chips, small stats): ALL ornament removed — the single manuscript gesture allowed is one 2px corner dot. Any container that cannot earn its grade drops to the next lower one; there is no penalty for a bare frame.

## Illumination on parchment walls vs umber-black panels
On parchment (#F5E9D1 wall): the weave is MONOCHROME — gold over-straps (#C9962E at 0.85) woven over the parchment surface itself (recess = the wall, no ink). The pattern RE-CEDES into the wall; only the inner rule is dark ink, so the frame reads as "the one drawn line." Contrast of strap to wall ≈ 2.4:1 — deliberately a whisper. On umber-black panels (#15090B): over-straps pale gold #E5C193 at 0.9; recess straps mid-umber #573928 at 0.8 (the authentic umber-under-gold); inner rule parchment at 0.5. The weave on a painting is brighter because the panel is the focal point.

## Light vs dark theme (same geometry, different material — never less decoration)
Light theme: wall #F5E9D1, panels #15090B; the panels are the "paintings." Dark theme: wall drops to deep umber #0D0508, so panels CANNOT stay #15090B (they'd vanish) — panels LIFT one step to #1B1114 and are separated by their gold frame alone; a 1px #E5C193@0.35 outline is added only where a panel must sit on a same-tone region. Wall hairlines go ghost (#FCF9F3 at 0.12). Weave swaps to the pale-gold family (#E5C193 at 0.75) over the void recess #0D0508 — gold threads over darkness, which is the manuscript-in-dark behavior. Accents always use the lifted variants on dark (#7BC9A8 verdigris, #E8836F red, #E8C46A saffron).

## Inscription motion (replaces frameIn's translateY card-slide)
Total ≤1.5s. Sequence: (1) inner rule draws L-to-R along the top then down both sides — stroke-dashoffset, 700ms, cubic-bezier(0.25,0.1,0.25,1); (2) weave band fades 0→1, 500ms, delay 350ms, NO translation (it appears, it does not slide — a pen deposits ink, a card slides); (3) rivet outline draws, 250ms, delay 900ms; (4) rivet dot scales 0→1 with gentle overshoot, 150ms cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1), delay 1100ms; content reveals 50ms after the inner rule completes. Stacked panels stagger 80ms apart. prefers-reduced-motion: collapse everything to a single 200ms opacity fade — no draw, no overshoot. This is the signature element; the loader stays the only continuous motion on screen.

## Subtlety rules (when a frame is warranted; anti-flag)
1. Frame presence is a DATA signal: only live instruments earn Grade 2+; static chrome earns Grade 0/1. A frame carrying no live content is decoration — this one rule deletes most of the "horrendous."
2. One instrument per container: weave band OR rivet OR accent thread — never all three at Grade 2.
3. Air is the ornament: the waste gaps and gutter carry more craft than any strap. When in doubt, remove a strap.
4. No flag/cloth, ever: no green-yellow-red adjacency (the palette's saffron/verdigris/madder are NEVER co-present in one frame — that is the Ethiopian flag), no repeating straight vertical stripes, no tricolor bands. The weave is continuous interlace; accent threads are sparse and never a trio.
5. Crisp strokes only: 1px or 2px, never 1.5px (retina aliasing — the current corners break this).
6. The diamond is a moment, not a motif: the rivet lozenge exists only at frame corners; the spinning "gem" divider is deleted (its job is done by the weave and the eye-dot).
7. Motion is reserved: inscription on load, ticker when live, eye-dot states, and the weave loader — four motions, nothing else animates.


### Motion & animation language — weave loader, boot/frame-inscription choreography, data micro-interactions, scroll & reveal, reduced-motion + performance strategy

**Verdict:** The current execution is decoration, not choreography. Concretely: (1) The loader is three pigment triangles on a free circular orbit — a pinwheel. There is no shared path, no over/under interlacing, no weave ripple, and no terminal state, so the triangles never "weave" anything, cannot resolve into the woven diamond mark, and read as a generic spinner. (2) The manuscript frame is a single flat stroke with floating corner diamonds and a dense chevron/lattice band: the diamonds carry no architectural job (no miter return, no keyline, no pinning onto the rule), the lattice is a texture sticker with no derivation from the double-rule manuscript system, and there is no air gap or layered rule, so the frame has zero depth. (3) There is no easing/timing system — motion is flat or evenly-bouncy, uncoupled from meaning, with no tokens, no settle-to-rest, no stagger discipline. (4) The boot has no sequence: loading, frame, and content are not arranged into the weave-settle → frame-inscription → reveal arc the identity line promises, and the "inscribes on load" signature is not actually timed. (5) There is no reduced-motion or performance discipline: nothing is transform/opacity-only, alerts strobe indefinitely, and no compositor-layer or repaint budget exists. Governing rule for the fix: every motion must (a) derive from weave/frame/ink metaphors, (b) decelerate into rest or travel constantly — never bounce, (c) terminate in a static resolved state (the mark, the live eye-dot, the underlined alert), and (d) move one thing at a time, sequentially, not all at once.

0. EASING & DURATION TOKENS (single source of truth)
═══════════════════════════════════════════
--ease-ink:    cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)   // the settle-into-the-page ease. Decelerate to rest. Used for ALL entrances, rolls, reveals, ignitions, and the loader's settle. NO overshoot, NO bounce, ever.

--ease-thread: linear                            // continuous travel: weave strand travel, ticker ribbon, scroll-fill rail. A thread being pulled is constant; only a spinner eases.

Color semantics: gold = live/existence; pigments (verdigris/saffron/madder) = events and loading; umber = rest. Motion never invents new colors — it uses the palette's event hues.

1. THE WEAVE LOADER (refined) — interlace, not orbit
═══════════════════════════════════════════
The fix is to make the three strands BRAID on one shared path with over/under crossings, instead of rotating as a pinwheel.

GEOMETRY:
- Tile: 96×96px. One shared lozenge path (a 45° diamond, not three separate orbits):
  vertices N(0,-34) E(24,0) S(0,34) W(-24,0) in px; each corner rounded r=7px. Side ≈41.6px, perimeter P≈166px.
- Strands: THREE kites (needle wedges, calligraphic — this is what fixes "horrendous triangles"), phase-offset 120° starting at N, SE, SW. Each kite: 26px long along travel, 6px wide at tail base, tapering to a sharp point at the head (leading). Opacity ramp 0.95 at head → 0.20 at tail tip (the brushstroke taper — a drawn stroke of pigment, not a filled triangle). Flat pigment fill + a 0.75px inner highlight at +18% luminance (varnish depth).
- Pigments on parchment boot: verdigris #1E8A5E · saffron #E8A33D · madder #A62F1E. On umber-black panels (in-page loaders): light variants #7BC9A8 · #E8C46A · #E8836F.
- WEAVE RIPPLE (the thing that makes it a weave): each strand's centerline = base path + normal-offset sine, amplitude 1.6px, frequency 3 per cycle → strands cross 6×/cycle at exactly 400ms spacing. At each crossing, handedness z-order resolves (strand A over B over C over A); the under-strand drops to opacity 0.55 and luminance −8% for the 120ms pass (classic interlace shadow). The crossing is the loader's perceptual beat — 6 beats per 2400ms.
- INK TRAIL: a 0.9px residue stroke along the path ahead of each head, fading 0.5→0 opacity over 600ms (the pigment staining the strand, cooling as it passes).

TIMING: cycle 2400ms, travel constant (linear — a thread, not a spinner). Ripple and trail give the rhythm, not the travel.

SETTLE (the loader's PURPOSE): on content-ready, a single 0→1 progress tween over 400ms (ease-ink) decelerates travel 100→0 and damps the ripple 1.6→0.6px, converging the three strands into the static woven-diamond mark (§2). The loader terminates into the mark — it never spins forever.

VARIANTS:
- Loading: full pigment, 2400ms cycle, ink trail on.
- Empty: same geometry, MUTED — strands at 38% opacity, no ink trail, cycle slowed to 3200ms. Reads as "waiting," not "working."
- Error: the weave UN-WEAVES. Verdigris and saffron strands fade to 20% opacity (600ms, ease-ink); madder strand reverses direction on the shared path (ease-press), ripple amplitude doubles to 3.2px so the crossings become near-misses (the knot coming apart), cycle 3200ms. Error = the weave unraveling, meaning-bound.
- Live: no loader. The mark is static; the gold eye-dot at its center pulses at 2400ms (scale 1.000→1.004 over 2400ms ease-ink, a breathing, not a blinking). Alive but still.

KEYFRAMES (drivers):
@keyframes strand-travel { from { offset-distance: 0% } to { offset-distance: 100% } } /* linear, 2400ms */
@keyframes strand-ripple { /* normal-offset sine, 3 cycles, 1.6px, applied per-crossing z/shadows at 400ms marks */ }
@keyframes settle-weave { /* 400ms ease-ink, drives travel speed→0, ripple 1.6→0.6px */ }
@keyframes ink-trail { from { opacity:.5 } to { opacity:0 } } /* 600ms */
@keyframes eye-ignite { from { opacity:.18; transform:scale(.6) } to { opacity:1; transform:scale(1) } } /* 260ms ease-ink */

### Color system & tokens

**Verdict:** The palette is delivered as flat, full-chroma swatches with no tonal ramp, no neutral hierarchy, no semantic mapping, and no derivation method. Four failures are provable: (1) every pigment is used at its single core value everywhere, so madder/verdigris/saffron/gold collide at equal visual weight and nothing recedes — there is no 60/30/10 structure, which is why the ornament (diamond, lattice, loader triangles) reads as "horrendous": flat equal-weight accent fills give every shape the same shout level. (2) The two most load-bearing accents FAIL WCAG AA as text on the parchment wall: verdigris #1E8A5E on #FCF9F3 = 4.12:1 (under 4.5) and gold #C9962E on #FCF9F3 = 2.53:1 — so a "LIVE" label or a rising rate rendered in these would be illegible on the gallery wall. (3) The dark-panel variants (#7BC9A8, #E8836F, #E8C46A) are pastel lifts that desaturate toward websafe mint/coral/pale-yellow and break the manuscript's warm chroma — they read as a generic health dashboard, not illuminated pigment. (4) There is no disabled or neutral state, indigo is undifferentiated from ink, and no per-pigment allowed/forbidden scope, so state colors stack onto the same saturated values. The cure is tonal discipline: one accent per viewport at <=10%, accents at their 700-step on parchment and 300-tint on ink, gold demoted from a flat accent to a scarce gilded signature, and tints/shades blended toward Parchment 300 and Ink 900 (never white/black, which is what grayed the pigments out).

**Spec:**

# COLOR SYSTEM & TOKENS — Ethiopian Classical Design Language

## 0. Derivation method (the one rule that fixes everything)
Tints and shades are NOT mixed toward white/black (that desaturates and "websafes" the pigments). Every step is an sRGB blend toward the two manuscript neutrals that already carry warm character:
- Light steps blend toward **Parchment 300 #F5E9D1**
- Dark steps blend toward **Ink 900 #15090B**
Ratios fixed across ALL pigments: 100 = 12% pigment + 88% parchment · 300 = 45% + 55% · 500 = 100% pigment (core) · 700 = 55% pigment + 45% ink · 900 = 30% pigment + 70% ink. Because parchment and ink are themselves warm, the scale stays chromatic at every step instead of graying out.

## 8. 60-30-10 applied
- LIGHT: 60% ground = parchment 100/300/500 walls + cards · 30% secondary = the umber-black data panels (the "paintings") + umber structure/borders + neutral text · 10% accent = gold + at most ONE of madder/verdigris/saffron; gold capped at ~3%.
- DARK: 60% ground = ink 900 canvas + umber 900 raised · 30% = panel wells (#0D0508), parchment text hierarchy, hairlines · 10% = same accent rule.
Golden rule: max two chroma families in any viewport — gold always counts as one.

## 9. Restraint rules (where each pigment is ALLOWED)
1. Gold: ONLY the eye-dot, the "LIVE" word, the single 2px gilded knot per frame, the boot-screen glyph, one header hairline, weave tips. Never a fill, never bulk text.
2. Verdigris: ONLY "connection/stream healthy" and positive rate direction (steady-state weave color). No decorative use.
3. Madder: ONLY alert/negative states and the weave's single failing segment. Never in lattice or dividers.
4. Saffron vs ochre are mutually exclusive: ochre = URGENT, saffron = CAUTION. One per viewport.
5. Indigo: shadows under panels, depth in the weave, panel cavity — never text on parchment, never a state.
6. Umber: all structure — frames, dividers, muted text, panel borders. It is the workhorse.
7. Parchment: grounds and holds text only; never decorative.
8. Accent size caps: fills no larger than the eye-dot (<=12px) or a 1–2px rule; on parchment, accents appear only as 700-step text, 4px dots, or hairlines — never as fills.
9. The ONLY allowed gradients are parchment-to-parchment wall washes and ink-to-umber panel depth. Chroma gradients (madder-to-gold, etc.) are forbidden.
10. Ornament-color coupling (why the shapes felt horrendous): the lattice renders in umber 300 1px hairlines with NO fill; the corner diamond is an OUTLINE in umber 700 with one gold knot at its center, top-left only; the weave loader carries exactly one chroma triangle (the active one) — the other two are umber 300 outlines. Quiet geometry comes from scarce color, not lighter shapes.


### Typography system — bilingual editorial/terminal type for the Ethiopian Classical Design Language

**Verdict:** The execution violates its own founding principle. DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md promises "one consistent scale, strict ladder, order through proportion," but the specimen ships 17 bespoke sizes (0.62/0.64/0.66/0.7/0.72/0.74/0.78/0.8/0.95/1.15/1.35rem, 17px body, clamp(2.1→3.6rem)) with no module, no shared steps, and nine distinct letter-spacing values (.005→.2em). Concretely: (1) Bela Bereka is set at 0.62rem/0.78rem/1.15rem — all below its 22px optical floor — and rendered UPPERCASE with .2em tracking, which breaks its calligraphic joins and doubles its presence; the face appears 5× on one page (kicker, wordmark, h1, dropcap, boot word) against the "one memorable thing" rule. (2) Uppercase + wide tracking is applied to nine elements (kicker, chip, meta, dateline, figcaption, panel title, live label, note, footnote) — when everything is a label, nothing is; hierarchy is flattened, which is exactly the "no subtlety" the client hears. (3) Ge'ez is bolted on as colored mono `.gez` spans with ad-hoc 0.5rem margins and zero metric engineering: one line-height (1.75) is applied to both scripts, so Ethiopic fidel with diacritics either clips or the Latin reads airy; inline accents have no baseline-join rule and no leading compensation. (4) The rates table has no head row, no decimal-alignment or sign grammar, and every delta wears ▲▼ + color — the flat-execution smell; triangles should be ticker-only. (5) There is no article-vs-terminal voice split: the terminal view (rates/ticker/feed) reuses article chrome instead of collapsing to a calm near-monochrome 11px/13px ladder. Fixing these five is the difference between "horrendous shapes" and a manuscript that reads as inevitable.

**Spec:**

## THE SYSTEM — two ladders, three faces, two shared steps
Faces: Bela Bereka 700 (display only, min 1.375rem/22px) · Noto Sans Ethiopic 400/500/700 (body/UI/labels — the only face permitted below 1rem) · HH Lemd Mono 400 (all numerals, all Latin data, timestamps, tabular) · Noto Sans Ethiopic 400 (Ge'ez inside mono/data context only, height-matched to Lemd). Grid: 4px base (0.5rem); every space is a multiple of 4. The two ladders pass through the same two steps — 11px (label/head) and 13px (data/small) — so a number can sit inside prose and a prose label can crown a table without breaking rhythm. That crossing is the "one consistent scale."

## 1. EDITORIAL LADDER (article view) — base 16px, ratio 1.25, hero widens 1.333
- `--fs-h1` clamp(2.5rem,5.2vw,3.75rem) = 40–60px · lh 1.05 Lat / 1.16 Amh · tracking 0 · Bela 700 · max-width 30ch Latin / 24ch Ethiopic, text-wrap:balance. ONE per view.
- `--fs-h2` clamp(1.75rem,3vw,2.375rem) = 28–38px · lh 1.1/1.2 · Bela 700. Max 2 per view (section titles only where the gallery framing does not already carry the divide).
- `--fs-lead` (dek) clamp(1.125rem,1.7vw,1.3125rem) = 18–21px · lh 1.55/1.65 · Noto 400 · max-width 40rem.
- `--fs-body` 1.0625rem = 17px · lh 1.7 Lat / 1.85 Amh · Noto 400 · measure 42rem max (≈45 Lat chars / ≈35 Ethiopic chars); 17px stays because Ethiopic fidel need the room.
- `--fs-small` 0.8125rem = 13px · lh 1.5/1.6 · Noto 400 · captions, bylines, footnotes, figcaptions.
- `--fs-label` 0.6875rem = 11px · lh 1.4 · tracking 0.14em · Noto 700 UPPERCASE · the ONLY uppercase/tracked token: kicker, chip, meta, panel title. One line, always.
- `--fs-micro` 0.625rem = 10px · reserved, DISCARDED by default — current 0.62/0.64/0.66/0.72/0.74 all collapse into `--fs-label`.
- Pull-quote clamp(1.375rem,2.4vw,1.75rem) = 22–28px · lh 1.4/1.5 · Bela 700 · hairline gold rules 1px above/below, 1.5rem inset.
- Dropcap: Bela 700, 3.2em (~54px), color var(--gold-deep) on parchment (NEVER madder — red reads "alert" in a market terminal and would collide with the alert eye-dot), float left, lh 0.8, `text-indent:0.5ch`. Only the first paragraph.

## 2. TERMINAL LADDER (rates/tables/ticker/console) — base 13px mono, pixel-calm
- `--ts-hero` clamp(2.5rem,4.5vw,3.25rem) = 40–52px · lh 1.05 · HH Lemd 400 · tabular · tracking 0 · color panel-gold #F8E6B8 on umber-black · THE one big number = the eye-dot's readout. Delta beneath at `--ts-data`.
- `--ts-rate` 1rem = 16px · lh 1.5 · Lemd 400 tabular · active row / panel headline value.
- `--ts-data` 0.8125rem = 13px · lh 1.6 · Lemd 400 tabular · every table cell, every terminal figure.
- `--ts-tick` 0.8125rem = 13px · lh 1.5 · Lemd 400 · ticker values (the ticker is 11px label + 13px value, nothing else).
- `--ts-head` 0.6875rem = 11px · lh 1.5 · Noto 700 uppercase, tracking 0.12em · column heads, panel titles, terminal labels · color var(--panel-mut) #B99A6E.
- `--ts-term` 0.875rem = 14px · lh 1.6 · Lemd (Lat) / Noto Sans Ethiopic (Ge'ez) · console/log lines.
- THE RULE: the terminal view contains nothing but `--ts-head` (11) and `--ts-data` (13), plus `--ts-hero` reserved for the single live number. Any third size requires justification. Bela Bereka is BANNED from the terminal (except the masthead wordmark). The terminal's calm IS its hierarchy.

## 3. HIERARCHY — article vs terminal
Article: signal through SIZE+WEIGHT only; uppercase+tracking reserved to the one `--fs-label` step; hierarchy = h1 (display) → pull-quote (display, optional) → lead → body → small; 2 display moments max per scroll-viewport.
Terminal: no display face; hierarchy = the shared 11/13 crossing, elevated by ONE `--ts-hero` number. Table: `--ts-head` column row (sticky), numeric columns `text-align:right` (decimal-aligned), text columns left, row min-height 1.875rem (30px) with cell padding 0.375rem 1.5rem.

## 4. Ge'ez/Latin pairing — metric engineering (the depth the client wants)
- HEADLINE: one language at a time (data-lang toggle). NEVER mix scripts at display size. The permanent bilingual pair is only the wordmark: `ማለዳ` in Noto Sans Ethiopic + `Maleda` in Bela 700 at 1.25rem, joined by a 0.6em gap, no baseline shift.
- SUBHEAD/CHIP: both scripts in Noto 700 at the same size, space-separated with the 0.5em word-gap (e.g. "Markets · Exchange" / "ገበያ · ምንዛሪ").
- BODY: inline Ge'ez accent inside Latin prose — set at 1em, natural baseline, ink color (NEVER an accent color in body), `font-weight:500`, term glossed once, and the containing paragraph uses `--lh-am`. Rule: any line carrying Ge'ez takes the Amh leading.
- DATA: Ge'ez inside the mono context (ዶላር, ዩሮ…) set in Noto Sans Ethiopic at **1.04em of the Lemd size** with `vertical-align:-0.06em` so the fidel band optically matches Lemd's cap/x-band, `margin-right:0.4em`, color var(--panel-mut) on panels. This replaces the current ad-hoc 0.5rem `.gez` spans.
- LEADING MULTIPLIER: Amh = Latin × 1.09 (body), × 1.11 (display). In this always-bilingual product, default EVERYTHING to `--lh-am`; use the tighter `--lh` only for pure-Latin literals (USD/ETB, timestamps). This is the single biggest fix for the "horrendous" feel — today one 1.75 is applied to both scripts.
- FLOOR: no Ge'ez below 0.6875rem (11px). Below 11px the 26 fidel + 7 vowel-row marks become illegible; 0.62–0.66rem contexts must not carry Ethiopic.

## 5. NUMERALS + TABULAR DATA (HH Lemd Mono grammar)
- Every figure: `font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums lining-nums` (+ `font-feature-settings:"tnum" 1,"lnum" 1` if Lemd exposes them). No other figure styles.
- RATES: always 2 decimals (`128.40`). DELTA: signed mono string `+0.8` / `−0.3` using the true minus U+2212; the SIGN IS THE GLYPH — drop ▲▼ from all tables (ticker only, where motion earns them); color the whole value verdigris #7BC9A8 / red #E8836F on umber, never the sign alone.
- ALIGNMENT: right-align numeric cells at the decimal (mono makes this exact); unit tokens (`ETB`, `/lb`, `%`) at 0.72em of the figure, baseline, muted ink, non-breaking, no gap inside the value string.
- GROUPING: ASCII comma always (`128,400`) — copy-safe and aligns.
- DATES: Latin digits + Ge'ez month + Latin year (`9 ነሐሴ 2026`) in HH Lemd; Ethiopic numerals (፩–፱) only for ceremonial/ordinal contexts, never in data.
- THE HERO RATE (eye-dot readout): `--ts-hero` HH Lemd 400, panel-gold #F8E6B8 on umber, tracking 0, delta beneath in `--ts-data` colored by sign.

## 6. EDITORIAL VOICE OF BELA BEREKA — restraint, made law
USE: the one article h1 · one pull-quote per long piece · the masthead wordmark (bump floor to 1.25rem) · the boot-screen word.
NEVER: below 22px (today it runs 10–19px) · uppercase + tracking (today's .2em kicker is a contradiction — Bela is calligraphic, tracking breaks its rhythm) · in the terminal · for numerals (HH Lemd owns every number, even big ones) · more than twice per scroll-viewport (today: 5×) · as a dropcap in madder (use gold-deep).
VERDICT-CARRYING EXACT: current `.kicker` at 0.78rem/.2em upper/Bela must become `--fs-label` (11px Noto 700 upper 0.14em); the wordmark floor becomes 1.25rem.

## 7. SPACING / METRICS (exact)
- Grid 4px; spacing steps 4·8·16·24·32·48·64.
- Baseline grid: a strict shared baseline is impossible with Ethiopic variable metrics — leading is metric-driven (the multipliers above), and all blocks/margins align on 8px. State this in the doc so the team stops forcing 1.75.
- Prose column 42rem max; paragraph gap 1.25em (Latin) / 1.35em (Amh, for descender room); dek↔meta 1.5rem; head→dek 1.25rem; figure→prose 2.25rem.
- Panel: padding 1.25rem 1.5rem 1.5rem; table row min-height 1.875rem; cell padding 0.375rem 1.5rem (13px×1.6=20.8px lh + 4.6px air).
- Ticker band height 2.5rem (label 11px + value 13px, centered).
- Letter-spacing: the system permits exactly three values — 0 (display/body), 0.02em (small/caption/figures), 0.14em (uppercase labels only). Nine values today; collapse.
- Script-toggle word-gap 0.5em; labels max one line; every text-wrap:balance at display sizes; `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps ticker/loader inert but NEVER kills tabular alignment.


### Live-data information design — the rates/news terminal's data views: states, micro-interaction, sparkline, grid typography, painting framing, hierarchy

**Verdict:** The client is right, and the diagnosis is specific. (1) The signature shapes are placeholders, not geometry. ### Weave-tile rendering fidelity + scale-collapse ladder

**SVG construction (Grade 2, 16px band).** Author at 1:1 CSS px, no transforms after authoring (any scale → 1.5px aliasing). Clip a `<g>` to the band rect. Two strap families at +45°/−45°, perpendicular spacing S=8 (T/2); each strap a full-band `<line>`, stroke-width 1, `stroke-linecap="butt"`. **Never tile a pattern cell** — the crossing lattice has an irrational period (8√2), so pattern seams produce bright L-shaped moiré; full-length strokes have no seam.

Layer 1 (recess): both families solid, recess color. Layer 2 (over): both families dashed — the weave is interlace, both families carry over-segments. Crossing spacing along a strap = S√2 = 11.31px. Over/under is a global checkerboard (family+strap index even → over), so a given strap's under-crossings are every OTHER crossing: period P = 2·S√2 = 22.63px. Gap = 2px centered on each under-crossing (1px each side). Per family: `stroke-dasharray="20.63 2"` (P−2 over-run + 2px gap), `stroke-dashoffset="21.63"` for the parity family whose reference crossing is over, `"32.94"` (=+11.31) for the other. General formula: dash=P−2, gap=2, offset=(P/2−1)+parity·(P/2). (3x: S=6 → P=16.97, dash=14.97, offset 7.49/15.46.)

**Moiré / 1px / retina.** Strap ≤ T/8 (1 ≤ 2 ✓); air between strap edges ≥ 2× strap (7 ≥ 2 ✓). A 6px band is three 1px straps with 2px air — never one 6px strap (strap≈band≈repeat → checkerboard smear at high DPI). Keep vector (re-rasterizes per frame; never cache a scrolling bitmap); the weave never animates. `shape-rendering="geometricPrecision"` — never `crispEdges` (jagged diagonals). No 1.5px strokes anywhere. DPR 2/3 native: 1px strap = 2/3 device px.

### Ge'ez/Latin metric-matching sub-system: baseline-join and size-adjust engineering

## Tokens (single source of truth)
- Leading multipliers: `--lh` (Latin) and `--lh-am` (Amh). Amh = Lat × **1.09 body** / × **1.11 display**. Resolved pairs: body 1.7/1.85 · small 1.5/1.64 · lead 1.55/1.71 · h1 1.05/1.16 · h2 1.1/1.22 · ts-data 1.6/1.74 · ts-term 1.6/1.74. Default EVERYTHING to `--lh-am`; use `--lh` only for pure-Latin literals (USD/ETB, timestamps, numerals).
- Data-accent join: Ge'ez inside mono context = `font-family:Noto Sans Ethiopic, weight 400, font-size:1.04em` of the Lemd em, `vertical-align:-0.06em`, `margin-right:0.4em`, `color:var(--panel-mut)` on panels, ink otherwise. Derived value at 13px: fidel renders 13.52px, shifted down 0.78px so the fidel band sits optically on Lemd's baseline.
- Gloss saffron: `--saffron #E8B85A` (distinct from gold-deep; never madder).

## Baseline-join geometry
Join on the **baseline**, never x-height. Noto Sans Ethiopic is height-matched to Lemd's cap/x-band at 1.04em; the −0.06em nudge is a fixed offset, not a line-height derivative, so it holds at every step of both ladders (10→60px). At display sizes Ge'ez never joins Latin — single-language rule; the only permanent pair is the wordmark: `ማለዳ` Noto Sans Ethiopic + `Maleda` Bela 700 at 1.25rem, 0.6em gap, baseline-aligned, zero vertical shift.

## Inline-gloss rule (body)
Inline Ge'ez accent inside Latin prose: exactly **1em** (no 1.04 adjust — ink text, weight 500, ink color, never accent). First occurrence glossed once with 1px saffron underline, `text-underline-offset:0.2em`, `text-decoration-thickness:1px`. The saffron underline fires **at most once per article**; all later occurrences carry no underline. The containing paragraph takes `--lh-am` (any line carrying fidel uses Amh leading).

## Ethiopic floor
**0.6875rem (11px)** is the Ge'ez floor. `--fs-label` (11px) and `--ts-head` (11px) are legal with fidel; `--fs-micro` 10px is discarded — no Ethiopic below 11px anywhere.

### Terminal numeral/tabular grammar

TERMINAL NUMERAL / TABULAR GRAMMAR — executable spec. Tokens reference the terminal ladder only.

1. FIGURE FEATURES. Every figure sets `font-family:'HH Lemd Mono'; font-weight:400; font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums lining-nums; font-feature-settings:"tnum" 1,"lnum" 1;`. No proportional figures, no slashed zero, no other figure style.

2. CELL GEOMETRY (11/13 crossing). Data row: `--ts-data` 13px, lh 1.6. Head row: `--ts-head` 11px, Noto 700 uppercase, tracking 0.12em, `--panel-mut` #B99A6E. The crossing: an 11px label always crowns a 13px value in the same cell group; on the whole terminal ladder these two steps plus `--ts-hero` are the only sizes. Table `border-collapse:collapse`; row `min-height:1.875rem` (30px); cell padding `0.375rem 1.5rem`; rows split by 1px hairline `--panel-rule` at 12% alpha, no zebra. Numeric cells `text-align:right` — mono + tabular makes the decimal the true alignment anchor; text columns left. Cells never wrap.

3. SIGNED-DELTA GRAMMAR. Delta is one complete string: `+0.8` / `−0.3` / `0.0`. True minus U+2212 only, never hyphen-minus; the sign is always present, positive carries `+`. Color the WHOLE value: up `--delta-up` verdigris #7BC9A8, down `--delta-down` red #E8836F, flat `--panel-mut` — on umber, never sign-only. No ▲▼ inside tables; triangles appear only in the ticker, where motion earns them. `--delta-down` doubles as the alert eye-dot color; dropcap gold, not red.

4. RATE RULE. Always 2 decimals: `128.40`; trailing zero kept; the decimal point is the alignment anchor across the column. Never 0, 1, or 3 places in tables.

5. UNIT TOKENS. Units at `font-size:0.72em` of the figure (13px → 9.36px), baseline-aligned, `--panel-mut`, `white-space:nowrap`, no space inside the value string: `128.40ETB`, `/lb`, `%`. One token per figure.

6. GROUPING. ASCII comma always: `128,400`. Copy-safe, decimal-aligns identically to ungrouped.

7. DATES. Latin digits + Ge'ez month + Latin year in Lemd: `9 ነሐሴ 2026`. Ethiopic numerals ፩–፱ are ceremonial/ordinal only, never data.

8. --ts-hero EYE-DOT READOUT. `clamp(2.5rem,4.5vw,3.25rem)` = 40–52px, lh 1.05, Lemd 400, tabular, tracking 0, panel-gold #F8E6B8 on umber-black #17130F. Exactly one per view; delta beneath at `--ts-data`, sign-colored. Live tick: `@keyframes ts-hero-pulse{0%{color:#F8E6B8}12%{color:#FFF3CF}100%{color:#F8E6B8}}` applied once, 420ms, on value change — the only permitted blink; killed by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

9. TICKER BAND. Height 2.5rem (40px). Content: 11px label (`--ts-head`) + 13px value (`--ts-tick`, Lemd tabular) only; items split by 1px vertical hairline. Motion: content duplicated once; `@keyframes ts-ticker{from{transform:translateX(0)}to{transform:translateX(-50%)}}`; duration = `calc(clamp(30s, 0.55s × char-count, 60s))`; `animation-timing-function:linear`; pause on `:hover`; `prefers-reduced-motion` renders static — translate off, tabular alignment untouched. Flat delta in ticker uses `--panel-mut`; up/down get ▲/▼ glyphs at 0.72em before the value, same whole-value color.

10. STATES. Delta: up/down/flat. Row: idle / hover (bg umber-2 #231C15 at 50%). Hero: idle / pulsed 420ms. Ticker: running / hover-paused / reduced-motion static. All state transitions ≤300ms except the 420ms hero pulse.


### THE WEAVE LOADER + BOOT SEQUENCE

STAGE — SVG 44×44, viewBox 0 0 44 44, center c=(22,22). Sizes: 44px first-paint (centered in the panel's mat, no skeleton, no shimmer); 16px in the eye-dot slot during refetch (scale 0.364; stroke scales to 0.45px). Never a spinner ring, never orbiting dots.

STRAND GEOMETRY (exact) — Three tapered trapezoids; strand i spoke θ_i = 90°+120°i (y-down). Chord-bounded: wide outer base at r=19 spanning θ±72°; narrow inner flat at r=9 spanning θ±10°; both chords perpendicular to the spoke (hence parallel). Path M→L→L→L→Z, c=(22,22): O_L=c+19(cos(θ−72°),sin(θ−72°)); O_R=c+19(cos(θ+72°),sin(θ+72°)); I_R=c+9(cos(θ+10°),sin(θ+10°)); I_L=c+9(cos(θ−10°),sin(θ−10°)). Worked (i=0): (40.1,27.9)(3.9,27.9)(20.4,30.9)(23.6,30.9). Base chord 2·19·sin72°≈36.1px; inner flat 2·9·sin10°≈3.1px (the chopped tip); radial height 10px (r9→r19). Base 144°>120° spacing → 12° rim overlap each side: strands interleave, never pile. Fill = strand color 0.22; stroke = same color 1.25px round join — the full-opacity stroke rules the wash beneath, so over/under reads at every crossing. Tokens (parchment/panel): verdigris #1E8A5E/#7BC9A8, saffron #E8A33D/#E8C46A, madder #A62F1E/#E8836F. Fixed z-order verdigris→saffron→madder.

CENTROID SPIN — Each strand rotates +120° about its OWN centroid C_i=c+12.6(cosθ_i,sinθ_i); trapezoid centroid at radius 12.6 = 19−10(b_n+2b_w)/(3(b_w+b_n)). transform-origin:C_i. Keyframes over 2400ms: 0%→0°, 100%→120°, ease cubic-bezier(.65,.05,.28,.91), infinite. Phase 120° via negative delays: strand0 0ms, strand1 −800ms, strand2 −1600ms → a traveling wave, never a rigid pinwheel. Loop wrap is masked: strand i at 120° occupies strand i+1's rest slot while that neighbor is mid-flight — reads as the weave passing.

KNOT & LOZENGE — At t=0 and t=1200ms the three inner flats gather at closest center approach (r≈9) — the woven knot; phase the cycle's start offset so both land true. Gold lozenge: 10×10, rot 45°, 1px stroke, fill gold 0.15 (gold #E5C193 panel / #C9962E parchment), centered above strands. Opacity: 0→1 240ms @1080ms; dwell to 1560ms; 1→0 180ms to 1740ms. The diamond is woven by the pigments: appears just before the 1200ms knot, dwells through it, exits as it disperses.

BOOT (≈2.2s, dismissable) — 0–400ms empty parchment wall #FCF9F3. 400–1100ms strands draw in: stroke-dasharray P, dashoffset P→0, 700ms ease-out cubic-bezier(0,0,.2,1), +40ms/strand stagger. 700ms wordmark ማለዳ Maleda fades in (Bela Bereka 1.15rem, letter-spacing 0.14em). 1100ms rotation loop starts. 1080–1740ms first lozenge beat. 1500ms masthead mount hairline inscribes per-edge (1px #C9962E 0.85, inset 0): top 600ms@1500, right@1650, bottom@1800, left@1950, each cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1). 2200ms eye-dot loading→live; breath ring (7→14px, 0.12, 3600ms) starts. Any pointer-down during boot cancels remaining draws and reveals the UI.

REFETCH / EMPTY — Refetch: 16px loader in the eye-dot slot; top mount re-inscribes L→R 600ms ease-out; prior values held at 0.55 opacity (no skeleton, no layout jump); landing fades 0.55→1 over 320ms. Empty: a 14px resting lozenge at 0.6 only.

### THE PAINTING FRAME & INTERLACE SYSTEM

TOKENS. --mount:#C9962E@.85 · --mat-hair:rgba(248,230,184,.16) · --knot:#E5C193 stroke1.5px@.9 · --band:rgba(229,193,147,.5) stroke1px · --gap:2px surface color (panel #15090B / wall #FCF9F3) · --inscribe:cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1) · --ease-out:cubic-bezier(.2,0,.4,1). QUARTERFOIL KNOT (SVG 18×18, viewBox 0 0 18 18, centered on each corner ±9px). Single gold strand, stroke1.5 round, no fill, tracing r(θ)=7.2·cos(2θ): four petal loops on the 0/90/180/270° axes meeting at center (9,9), where the strand self-crosses four times. Center weave: crossing order clockwise = over/under/over/under; under-passages carry a 2px --gap, over-passages continuous (drawn last). Knots are the corner, replacing any floating diamond.

INTERLACE BAND (headpiece). SVG height 12, width = content width. Two strands y=6+3·sin(2πx/14+φ); strand A φ=0, strand B φ=π (half-period offset, exact mirror across y=6). FOOT LINE. 1px gold hairline (--mount@.6) full width; centered 6px lozenge (square rotated 45°, stroke --mount@.6, no fill) sits 4px above the hairline, 12px above the colophon note. Composition: head = active knot-work, foot = single lozenge at rest.

INSCRIPTION-ON-LOAD. First paint: frame rules inscribe clockwise — top 600ms, right @150ms, bottom @300ms, left @450ms; each stroke-dasharray = L L, dashoffset L→0, 600ms, --inscribe. @800ms four knots draw stroke-length 0→1 (500ms, --inscribe). @1200ms band inscribes left→right (700ms, --ease-out).
