# Character / Illustration / Animation Art-Style specs

Generated by the 15-agent character art-style fan-out.


## Characters · Illustration · Character Animation — the "Meskroch" (ምስክሮች, "the Witnesses") figure canon, modernized from Ethiopian icon & manuscript painting.

**Verdict:** The established language ships chrome and ritual — manuscript frame with rivet lozenges and interlace, the weave-loader, eye-dot states (gold live / madder alert / pulse loading), the flowing ticker, rubrication, Ge'ez chips — but has ZERO figure system. There is no canonical proportion, no figure palette (no skin ramp, no allowed cloth fields), no dimensioned almond-eye geometry, no pose or expression grammar, and no character animation timing. Any character produced today would be improvised, cartoon-adjacent, and would risk trivializing the sacred manuscript/icon source. This spec makes the figure a first-class, fully constrained system so characters read as gallery guardians — dignified, watchful, flat, frontal — never as mascots or decorations.

**Spec:**

## CHARACTER CANON — "THE MESKROCH" (ምስክሮች, "the Witnesses")

The canonical figures are the gallery's guardians: hieratic, frontal, almond-eyed figures who stand watch over the market as data flows. They are a *construction* built from the system's own geometry — never a likeness. Names in this spec are working labels for the design language, not branding.

ROLE RULES
- Figures appear ONLY in illustration, empty states, loading, and editorial moments. Never in chrome (no buttons, menus, nav, avatars), never as logo/mascot.
- Figures never occlude or touch data. The Herald may *gesture toward* a rate but the hand and data element keep ≥ 8px clearance.
- Figures are gender-neutral by default: the shamma/gabi head-cover hides the head, so no gendered hair, beard, or makeup. The Watcher is deliberately un-gendered and un-bearded.

### 1. PROPORTION SYSTEM (canon pane: 96×144, 2:3, figure centered on the x=48 axis)

Canonical unit: 1 nibus = the almond eye width = 5px at canon scale. All spacing derives from it.
- Head : body ratio = 1 : 8 (figure = 8 heads tall, the elongated hieratic canon). Head 18px tall, 15px wide.
- Shoulders 22px (4.4 nibus), torso tapers 22 → 14px, robe hem flares to 30px at y≈140.
- Arms 2px wide at the sides, 1–2px gap from the torso, hands at hip height (y≈70). Feet always hidden under the robe.
- Neck 3px, concealed under the shamma collar.
- Head interior (18px tall, measured from crown at y=0): hairline y=4 · brow line y=7 · eye box y=8–11 (eye midline y=9.5) · nose stroke y=10–13 · nose foot y=13–14.5 · mouth line y=15 · chin y=18.
- Secondary 1:5 bust (head-to-bust) is allowed ONLY in editorial figure captions ≥ 96px tall; never as a crop for chrome.

FACE / THE ALMOND EYE (the signature)
- Almond = 5px wide × 3px max height, drawn as two tangent arcs (inner canthus a point 1px lower than the outer canthus, which rises 1px — the classic icon slant). Inter-eye gap = 2px (0.4 nibus) — eyes close-set and large, the icon hallmark.
- Eye field = parchment #FCF9F3 bounded by 1px ink outline. Pupil = solid Ø2.5px, centered.
- PUPIL COLOR IS THE LIVE STATE: gold #C9962E = live/watching (default) · madder #A62F1E = alert · pulse gold→parchment = loading. This literalizes "paintings are content" — the Witness's eye IS the system's eye-dot. One 1px parchment catchlight dot at 2 o'clock on the pupil.
- Gaze: frontal and direct, pitched just past the viewer — the figures look *at but through* the terminal, watchful, never interrogating. The single authorized shift = "witnessing glance": both pupils move 1px to the SAME side, toward the illustrated datum. Both move together (cross-eyed and outward-staring are forbidden).
- Nose = abstract: 1px ink vertical stroke 10→13 with a 1.5px horizontal foot at 13–14.5. No shading, no wings, no nostril marks. Ears hidden by the shamma by default; if bare-headed, ears = 2×4px 1px ink arc.
- Mouth = 3px, 1px ink, neutral-straight with corners 0.5px low (serene, never smiling-by-default). Reserved micro-smile: corners lift 0.5px. Open mouths, teeth, tongues: forbidden.
- Brows: absent in the neutral register (icon-authentic). They appear ONLY in the Alert register as a 1px flat ink arch 1px above the eye box, width = eye width +1px. Never angled inward (no western angry V), never raised as cartoon arcs.

### 2. FIGURE PALETTE (the only pigments allowed on figures)

SKIN (flat fields only, all deep-umber, no other ethnicities):
- Skin Canonical #4A2813 — default figure on parchment/gold grounds.
- Skin Deep #2B1408 — dark-theme figures, background/second figures, and the back figure in two-figure scenes.
- Skin Ochre #8A5A2B — ONE reserved "lit" figure per editorial spread (the coffee-ceremony hostess, the herald on a saffron-rubricated page). Rare.

CLOTH (flat fields):
- Shamma/gabi: parchment #FCF9F3, 1px ink fold-lines, 3 parallel 1px ink stripes at the hem, optional 2px gold #C9962E band. Default garment.
- Robes/vestments: madder #A62F1E (dignified, rubric), verdigris #1E8A5E, deep ink robe #15090B with gold trim. Saffron #E8A33D is for trims/accents only — never a large cloth fill (too loud).
- Gold #C9962E is a metal/light: rims, borders, halo, band edges, pattern dots. Never a large fill.

HALO (modernized): flat gold disc Ø24px (1.6 × head width) behind the head = canonical. Thin 2px gold ring at Ø27px (no fill) = small/secondary figures and dark theme. Dark theme always uses parchment disc Ø24px + 2px gold ring at Ø27px so ink outlines stay legible. Halos belong to Witness / Watcher / Herald / Coffee-Hostess figures ONLY — crowd figures get none (hierarchy, and never trivialize the sacred).

### 3. LINE & RENDERING RULES

- Outline: silhouette 2px, interior details 1px, at canon 96px. Scale table: 192px → 3px silhouette / 2px interior; 48px → 1px all; ≤24px → glyph (below).
- Line color: light theme = ink #15090B (warm umber #3A1D10 allowed as an alternative); dark theme = parchment #FCF9F3 (the figure always sits on the parchment/gold disc, so ink interiors still read; silhouette flips to parchment).
- Flat fields, ZERO gradients, ZERO shading, ZERO drop shadows, ZERO inner highlights, ZERO noise. The only light = the flat parchment catchlight dot and the flat gold halo/rim.
- Fill-inset rule: every color region under-draws its outline by 1px so no ground-colored hairline shows between fill and stroke (kills the classic SVG gap artifact).
- No anti-aliasing fuzz: pixel-snap curves to the 1px grid; keep strokes on whole px.

### 4. EXPRESSION & POSE GRAMMAR

Default composure: serene watchfulness — full almond eyes, gold pupils, no brows, neutral mouth. "The composure of a ledger keeper."

EXPRESSION REGISTER (four discrete states, no blending, no cartoon faces):
- Watchful (default): full almond, gold pupil, no brows, neutral mouth.
- Alert: flat 1px brows appear, pupils → madder #A62F1E, mouth tightens to 2px. No wide eyes beyond the almond, no open mouth, no sweat drops.
- Serene (calm/settled): almond lowers to a resting crescent (lids at 60%), pupils gold, corners lift 0.5px. Used when the market settles / load completes.
- Herald (editorial "here is the number"): Watchful face + the open-palm raise. The hand speaks; the face stays neutral.

POSE SET (limited, meaningful — these are ALL):
1. Standing Witness (default) — frontal, arms at sides, palms slightly open, robe hem flared.
2. The Herald — right hand raised, palm OUT (fingers together, thumb out), hand at chest; left hand down. The open-palm "receiving/reporting" gesture, modernized from the icon blessing hand. Signature gesture.
3. The Keeper — hands folded at the waist, robe gathered in both hands. Calm, for idle/loading scenes.
4. The Coffee Hostess — seated at the mesob, one hand on the jebena, robe pooling in flat folds. Editorial ritual figure.
5. The Watcher (angelic) — halo MANDATORY, both palms raised to shoulder height, fingers together. The Debre Berhan Selassie homage for empty/loading states: "the market is being watched."

POSTURE RULES: primary figure always frontal, spine perfectly vertical, weight even (no contrapposto, no hip-pop, no slouch). Secondary figures may turn up to 3/4 frontal (≤20° head turn) but MUST retain both full frontal eyes (the stereoscopic-icon eye — authentic). Primary figures are NEVER in profile. No crossed arms, no hands in pockets, no thumbs-up, no pointing at the viewer, no legs apart, no relaxed stances.

### 5. ANIMATION (motion language for characters)

Principle: hieratic stillness is the default. Motion is confined, ceremonial, on a 12fps grid (1 frame = 1/12s), linear easing only — no bounce, no overshoot, no ease-elastic.

THE FOUR SANCTIONED LOOPS:
1. THE DRIFT (idle/waiting) — figure sways 1px laterally + 1° rotation, 1.6s loop, linear. Halo stays rigid (the figure breathes inside its frame; it does not wobble).
2. THE BLINK OF THE WITNESS (signature) — the almond eyes close to a 1px slit and reopen, 5 frames total (~0.42s): open → 60% lid → 20% lid → 1px closed line → reopen. Occurs every 4–6s. The gold pupil stays visible through the slit (it "glows through"). It reads as a shutter closing, not a blink-of-a-cartoon.
3. THE HERALD'S LIFT — raised palm lifts 4px (chest → chin) while the index finger extends; 0.6s in / 0.8s hold / 0.6s out. Fires once when data arrives, then returns to The Drift.
4. THE WATCH PULSE (alert) — pupils switch to madder AND the silhouette flashes madder 1px for exactly one 1.0s loop, then settles back to ink. Mirrors the red-alert eye-dot.

LOADING AFFORDANCE (when a figure doubles as the loading indicator): the figure is STATIC (never re-implements the weave-loader); loading is conveyed by (a) pupils pulsing gold→parchment in step with the eye-dot pulse, and (b) THE HALO DRAWS ITSELF — the gold ring's stroke sweeps 0→360° via stroke-dasharray, 1.2s loop, linear. Used only as loading; stops when content lands.

Appear/disappear: figures enter/leave via a 0.8s dissolve or a 1px cross-fade through the manuscript frame's interlace. No slides, no cut-away western style.

FORBIDDEN MOTION: walk cycles, jumping, spinning, squash-and-stretch on the body, swinging limbs, head-bobbing, finger-tapping, cape-flapping, any idle-loop energy. A Witness that "moves too much" breaks the canon.

### 6. SCALE / GLYPH RULES

- ≤24px: render as a glyph = halo-disc + two almonds + gold pupils only (no body).
- 24–48px: robe silhouette + eye details; no interior cloth folds.
- 48px+: full canon.
- Figures ship at 96–288px in empty states and editorial; the halo-glyph is the smallest sanctioned appearance. Faces NEVER appear in product chrome at any size.

### 7. RESPECT / CULTURAL RULES

- No direct copies: no reproduction of a specific icon, no tracing of Debre Berhan Selassie angels, no reference to a named saint's face. The figure is an abstract construction of almond arcs, trapezoid robes, and a circle.
- No caricature: never exaggerate features for comedy, never distort the umber skin, never render the figures in a mocking or kitsch register.
- Halo discipline: the halo is reserved for the four sanctioned figure types and never combined with a live ticker/chart in a way that trivializes the sacred form.
- The figures are the gallery — data is the painting. They stand in the margins, never overlaid on data, never decorated with market glyphs on their robes.
- Captions in Amharic use proper orthography (e.g., ምስክር for "witness") set in Noto Sans Ethiopic Ge'ez mono or Noto Sans Ethiopic — never transliteration.

### 8. DO / DON'T

DO: 1px/2px ink (parchment-on-dark) outlines; flat fields from the figure palette only; gold halo disc/ring; 1:8 elongation; the 5×3px almond with a gold live pupil; the open-palm Herald gesture; the shamma head-cover; frontal-only primary; 12fps ceremonial motion.
DON'T: big-head-cutie (chibi / 3-head) proportions; photoreal or gradient rendering; western cartoon clichés (cute dot-eyes, huge anime eyes, sweat drops, anger marks, wobbling outlines, speed-streak motion lines); 3/4 or profile primary figures; caricature; direct icon copies; any skin color outside the umber ramp; mascots/logos; faces in chrome; characters touching/occluding data; re-implementing the weave-loader as a figure.


## Illustration System & Scenes

**Verdict:** The language has motifs (eye-dot, cross-lattice, Ge'ez chips) and the "painting" thesis is asserted for data panels, but there is NO illustration system — and the existing restraint rules actively forbid one. Concretely: (1) Restraint rule 8 caps accent fills at ≤12px and limits every viewport to max 2 chroma families, so editorial/empty/onboarding art has no sanctioned space to exist as flat pigment fields; (2) there are no composition rules — no hieratic register system, no frontal-field law, no haräg/pattern vocabulary for backgrounds, so any scene would be drawn without structure; (3) there is no scene vocabulary (Addis street, mercato, buna, mesob, the ticker-world) and no character/scene anatomy; (4) there is no rule for on-parchment vs on-umber illustration, no gilt-line flip for linework on dark, and no treatment discipline (flat fields, no gradients, weave-as-texture at low opacity); (5) the "painting frames data" thesis stops at panels-as-paintings — there is no rule for how a painted SCENE coexists with live numbers without the painting animating or the numbers colliding with ornament. This spec fixes all five by defining a Painted-Field exception with its own pigment/pattern law, a Static/Living color-semantic (painted world = parchment/ink/umber, NEVER changes; live data = gilt/verdigris plane only), a five-scene vocabulary, and the gilt data-tablet (R2) as the terminal's signature illustration-data relationship.

# ILLUSTRATION SYSTEM & SCENES — "The Painted Field" (for Ethiopian Classical Design Language, grounding specs-deep-dive.md + DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md)

## 0. PROMISE & THE ONE LAW
An illustration is a PAINTING hung in the gallery (the terminal IS the gallery, the viewport IS the wall, every card IS a hanging painting). Illustration is the SANCTIONED EXCEPTION to the chrome restraint caps (fills >12px, >2 chroma families are normally forbidden): a painted field is one deliberate instrument, allowed exactly once per viewport, obeying its own stricter laws — no gradients, no blur/glow/shadows, no flag-trio, no new colors, no animated world.

THE STATIC/LIVING LAW (the core, load-bearing): within any illustration the painted world — parchment, umber, ink, and the two world pigments — is STATIC. It never changes color or position for data events. ALL live state lives in the gilt data plane: the gold-verdigris tablet, the ticker line, the eye-dot, the halo behind data. When a rate ticks: the world holds perfectly still; the tablet's number rolls; the eye-dot breathes. This is what keeps a busy painting calm and makes data legible against ornament.

## 1. COMPOSITION RULES
### 1.1 The frontal field
- Flat frontal only. No perspective, no vanishing point, no foreshortening, no atmospheric depth. Depth is SYMBOLIC: vertical register stacking = distance (upper = farther); scale = importance (hieratic), never proximity.
- Master module 24px (6× the 4px grid); all scenes snap. Standard canvases: editorial header 800×500 (16:10), card/empty square 480×480, boot/onboarding 800×500 (or 960×540).
- Three-register ground (y = % of canvas height): APEX 0–28% (sky, sun, halos, incense smoke, AND the digital/data plane); ACTION 28–68% (figures, jebena, mesob, market table, monitors); GROUND 68–100% (ground line, table, cups, goods, the grounding weave band).
- Band-cross rule: an object may cross exactly ONE register boundary (a jebena neck, a figure's head). Never two. Crossing = emphasis.
- Hieratic scale: at most three scale grades per scene — hero 1.6×, support 1.0×, accent 0.6×. The hero sits at the symbolic center (horizontal center, vertical center of the action register). Importance is expressed by scale AND centrality together, never one alone.
- Symmetry is preferred, never mandatory; a one-side-weighted scene must carry a counter-weight (second object, halo, frame element) on the other side. The focal point always lands on the vertical center line.
- Frame rule: everything lives inside the frame (manuscript frame Grade 1–2 for small paintings; the viewport edge for full-bleed). Max ONE deliberate break per scene (a sun ray, a bird's wing), and it must re-enter the field — a break is a beat, not a style.
- No cropping of figures at the frame (whole figures only, manuscript tradition). The ground register always contains the hero's base.

### 1.2 Symbolic order (hieratic)
- Read order is sacred-top-to-ordinary-bottom and center-first: sun/halo (sacred) → hero figure/object → goods/data.
- Number symbolism is real: 3 (the three rounds of buna, the three pigments, the three monitors), 4-fold symmetry for objects (mesob weave, quarterfoil), 7/12 for sacred counts. Use 3-cycles and 4-part symmetry; never 5s or 6s in hero positions.
- NO FLAG, extended from chrome to scenes: the three pigments (verdigris/saffron/madder) never sit in a row or triad. Max TWO world pigments per scene; the third position is always parchment/ink/gold. (Gold always counts as one chroma family.)

### 1.3 The haräg and geometric pattern vocabulary (reuses the established weave-tile laws: strap ≤ T/8, air ≥ 2× strap, over/under with a 2px gap in field color, full-length strokes never tiled — no seams)
- P1 Interlace band (the haräg) — scene frames + ground bands. Two-strand plait, strand y=6+3·sin(2πx/14+φ), A φ=0, B φ=π, crossings every 7px, over-strand continuous, under-strand 2px gap. Grade 2+ frames only; never a full-margin carpet (restraint).
- P2 Diamond lattice (mesob/weave) — 24×24 tile, kite strokes 1.5px. 100% for woven OBJECTS (mesob, baskets, mats, cup-weave); ≤10% as background texture.
- P3 Cross-tile (abstract Ethiopian cross, 4-fold rotational) — 40px tile, stroke 1.5px outline-only (never a Latin cross, never a filled cross). For umber-panel backgrounds and the gallery/onboarding world. ≤10%.
- P4 Quarterfoil — 4 petals meeting at a 12px center, tile 40px. Ceiling/garden scenes and panel texture. ≤10%.
- P5 Halo rings — concentric circles r 48/60/72 at 6%/4%/2% opacity behind hero heads and sacred objects.
- Scene rule: at most TWO of P1–P5 per scene. Background pattern never exceeds 10% (on parchment: ink at 8%; on umber: gold at 10%). Pattern is never animated.

## 2. SCENE PALETTE & THE TWO HOSTS
### 2.1 The closed pigment set (no new colors; established 100–900 scales)
- Figure/umber tones: umber 600 #4A2F20 (base skin/clay), umber 700 #573928 (shadow), umber 800 #3A2016 (deep shadow; the on-umber panel field). On-umber figures lift to umber 500 #6C523D (base) with umber 300 #D0B28E as detail.
- World pigments (allowed per scene, 300/500/700 steps only): madder #A62F1E (blood, coffee, clay, urgent), verdigris #1E8A5E (leaves, coffee gardens, connection), saffron #E8A33D (sun, coffee berries, ghee-light), gold #C9962E (halos, gilt, and the data plane). Max TWO world pigments + gold per scene. On umber hosts use the 300-tints (#94BE9D, #D19580, #EFCA8E, #E1C488) — the established "illuminated pigment" ramp. 100-tints never as fills; parchment is the ground, never a fill.
- Line colors: on-parchment = ink 900 #15090B (primary) and ink 900@0.65 (hairlines). On-umber = the GILT LINE: honey gold #E5C193@0.9 (primary), parchment #F8E6B8@0.7 (secondary), honey-gold@0.5 (pattern). Ink never draws on umber (it vanishes).

### 2.2 Parchment vs umber hosts
- ON-PARCHMENT (day): field parchment 100 #FCF9F3, ink 900 outlines, world pigments at 500 cores, gold 500 for data. High-key, airy. Editorial headers, most empty states, onboarding on light, and the mini-paintings hung in the gallery.
- ON-UMBER (lamplight): field umber 800 #3A2016 (never pure ink 950 — a painting needs a warm ground), gilt #E5C193 outlines, world pigments at 300-tints, gold 300 #E1C488 for data. Low-key, burnished. Loading/error scenes, the terminal's own world, onboarding on dark, and ALL data-bearing scenes (they are panels).
- The host is chosen by WHAT the illustration is, not by theme alone: data-bearing scenes go on umber (they're panels); narrative/editorial scenes go on parchment. One host per illustration — never mix fields in one painting. (Mini-paintings inside an on-umber gallery scene are legal objects: the gallery is full of paintings.)

## 3. RECURRING SCENE VOCABULARY (five canonical scenes, fixed anatomy — recurrence IS the system's memory; the jebena reappears in S3, S4, and the boot)
- S1 ADDIS STREET: flat frontal frieze of 3–5 facades (max), heights 60–100% of the action register, flat rectangles with window grilles cut from P2 lattice; one blue-white minibus (the "blue donkey") as the accent object; one tree or street lamp as the single vertical accent on one side. No receding streets — a frieze.
- S2 THE MARKET (mercato): frontal vendor stall, produce as stacked flat circles/teardrops in 2–3 of the allowed pigment steps, a flat triangular awning, woven baskets in P2 (100%), ground weave band beneath. A still-life register.
- S3 BUNA (coffee ceremony — the signature hospitality scene): the jebena (round-bellied clay pot, no handle, long upward spout) hero at 1.6×, clay umber 600 flat, one madder 500 rim band, ink 900 contour; set on three stones (shiferaw) in ink over a flat fire; three cini cups (no handles) in a line at 0.6× for the three rounds (abol/tona/baraka), sizes 0.55/0.6/0.65 — symbolic, never animated; the qetema grass spread as a flat green tongue; incense as two braided S-coils rising into the apex register.
- S4 THE MESOB: the woven basket table with domed lid, drawn ENTIRELY in P2 diamond lattice — the load/empty/waiting glyph in physical space (mirrors the weave loader). Three cups around it for the ceremony.
- S5 THE TICKER-WORLD (the figure watching screens — THE product scene): one hieratic frontal figure, dark-umber skin in flat field, large almond eyes, gaze forward; before them a row of 3 flat monitors drawn as gold-rimmed tablets whose faces carry ticker lines; a P5 halo behind the head; the figure's eye-dot mirrors the terminal's state. One figure max in ANY scene (restraint); faces appear only here and in editorial/empty/onboarding moments, never in product chrome.
- Object rules: every grounded object sits on a ground line (1.5px ink/gilt, or the P1 ground band); a flat ground shadow — hard-edged ellipse, ink 900 @25% (on umber: ink 950 @30%), offset +6px vertical — sits under every grounded object. Only halos, sun, incense, and the data plane float.

## 4. TEXTURE & TREATMENT
- Flat fills only. NO gradients inside a painted field — not even parchment washes (the wall wash stays a chrome property, never a painting property). Depth is made by: (a) the flat ground shadow, (b) the weave over/under, (c) the halo. No blur, no glow filters, no drop shadows.
- Line discipline (at 800px canvas; primary = canvasWidth × 0.00375 ≈ 3px; snap to nearest 0.5px; below 480px wide drop tertiary to 1px):
  - Primary contour 3px, ink 900 (on-umber: gilt #E5C193@0.9) — all figure/object outlines.
  - Secondary detail 2px, ink 900@0.8 (on-umber: parchment #F8E6B8@0.7) — features, folds, inner structure.
  - Tertiary/pattern 1.5px, ink 900@0.6 (on-umber: honey-gold@0.5) — lattice, weave, rays, texture. 1.5px is legal at painting scale; it is never legal in chrome (the scribe's hairline stays a chrome law).
- Fill technique — the manuscript "cut": contour line sits INSIDE the fill (on the boundary), never straddling; between two fills draw exactly ONE line (never a double line of both colors).
- Texture = ONLY P1–P5 at their specified opacities plus the weave. No paper grain, no noise, no brush texture. Pattern is a field texture (≤10%) or an object's actual weave (100%).

## 5. HOW ILLUSTRATION FRAMES DATA (the painting thesis, executable)
Three legal relationships:
- R1 THE FRAMED PAINTING: a small Grade 1–2 painted scene beside/above a data panel. The painting is memory/context; the panel holds the live data. Calm, editorial. Data never touches the painting's linework.
- R2 THE PAINTING HOSTS THE DATA — THE TERMINAL'S SIGNATURE ILLUSTRATION: the scene fills the space and INSIDE it sits a gilt data tablet (a cartouche/inscription tablet), drawn as a 1.5px gold 500 border on a parchment face, with Grade-2 rivet lozenge corners at painting scale (lozenge long axis 12px, dot 2px). The tablet is the ONLY element drawn in gold+verdigris and it FLOATS (data plane). Its eye-dot (12px; gold LIVE #C9962E / pale gold #E5C193 on umber; red ALERT #651E15 / #E8836F on umber; pulsing gold LOADING — exactly as specified) sits at the tablet's top-right, or at the frame's corner rivet.
- R3 THE PANEL IS THE PAINTING (full-bleed on-umber): a data panel whose face IS a painted scene at umber 800 with P2 weave at 8%, the data overlaid as the live gilt layer. The gallery thesis at full power — painting and panel are the same surface.
- The Static/Living Law (§0) applies to all three. ALERT: the world stays; the tablet's gold line shifts to madder; the eye-dot goes red — no ripple through the scene.
- Data placement law: numbers live ONLY on tablets or the ticker band — never written across a wall, a basket, or a cup.
- Budget: one illustrated area per viewport; two world pigments + gold max; gold in a scene ≤ the data plane + one halo.

## 6. SCENE STATES & ANIMATION (keyframes/timing; tokens --ease-ink, --ease-thread from the motion spec; all respect prefers-reduced-motion → scenes render static)
The painting is static; its DATA animates. Two legal scene motions only:
- M1 EMPTY/LOADING (the waiting painting): scene renders at full opacity; the data tablet is ABSENT; the eye-dot slot is the 16px weave loader. On data arrival the tablet inscribes and the world does not move.
  - @keyframes tablet-inscribe { from{stroke-dashoffset:L} to{stroke-dashoffset:0} } /* 700ms --ease-ink: top L→R, then both sides */
  - tablet reveal: opacity 0→1, 260ms --ease-ink (delay 700ms); eye-dot ignite 0.18→1 + scale(.6)→1, 260ms --ease-ink; then the existing 340ms number roll. The weave loader's 400ms settle resolves into the tablet's rivet eye-dot (the loader→live signature, reused).
- M2 THE BREATH (hero only — boot + the single hero editorial): halo breathes scale 1.000→1.004 over 2400ms --ease-ink alternate; sun rays/incense render at 2% scale. The painting is ALIVE but still.
  - @keyframes halo-breathe { from{transform:scale(1)} to{transform:scale(1.004)} } /* 2400ms --ease-ink alternate, boot/hero only */
- One legal transition: full-bleed editorial scenes cross-fade on section change — opacity 0→1, 600ms --ease-ink (no pan, no zoom, no parallax inside a painting).
- Reduced motion: scenes render static; loader/ticker/number-roll keep minimum function; no breath, no cross-fade motion, no inscription draw (tablet appears in one 200ms fade).

## 7. DO / DON'T
DO: render flat-frontal, hieratic, register-stacked, symmetry-favored. Use 3-cycles and 4-fold symmetry. Keep the painted world static; animate only the gilt data plane, the eye-dot, the loader. Use P2 at 100% for woven objects and ≤10% as background; P1 ground bands; P5 halos. Give every grounded object a flat ground shadow; one line between two fills; contour inside the fill. Use ink on parchment, gilt #E5C193 on umber. Make data always a gold/verdigris tablet or ticker — visually distinct from the painted world. One illustrated area per viewport; max two world pigments; gold counts as one.
DON'T: use perspective/foreshortening/atmospheric depth. Crop a figure at the frame. Float grounded objects (only halo/sun/incense/data float). Animate the painted world. Use gradients, blur, glow, or drop shadows in a painting. Put saffron+verdigris+madder together in one scene (the flag). Write data across walls/baskets. Render a cross (Latin or filled) — keep P3 outline-only. Multiply figures or faces (one hero figure max; faces only in editorial/empty/onboarding). Mix hosts in one painting.

## Character & figure animation grammar — locomotion, gaze, draw-in, idles, expression timing, data-reaction, reduced-motion

**Verdict:** The motion system in DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md §5 and specs-deep-dive.md covers only the chrome — weave loader, frame inscription, ticker ribbon, eye-dot states, scroll reveals — and its own governing rule ("derived from weave/frame/ink metaphors, decelerate into rest, terminate in a static resolved state, one thing at a time") has NO implementation for the illustrated figures the identity promises ("paintings are content"). Concretely absent: (1) no locomotion grammar — nothing defines how a figure with weight moves; "never bounce" is asserted but never given keyframes, eases, or amplitudes; (2) no gaze animation — yet the eye-dot's whole thesis is that the terminal's eye watches the data, and a figure is that eye given a body; (3) no character entrance/exit — the system says "a pen deposits ink, a card slides" for the frame, but there is no draw-in equivalent for a figure; (4) no data-reaction choreography — the rate-tick micro-interactions (roll 340ms, wash 440ms, flap 300ms) are defined for digits, but a watching figure has no defined response; (5) no reduced-motion form for characters ("static composed pose" is not specified). This pass defines the full grammar reusing the existing tokens so a figure moves with the same physics as the system: --ease-ink cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) (all arrives/settles/plants), --ease-press cubic-bezier(.7,0,.84,0) (all departures/withdrawals), --ease-thread linear (continuous travel: gaze pursuit, garment wave), the --dur-* ladder, and the eye-breath cadence (2400ms ease-ink alternate, scale 1→1.004) — a figure's breathing cycle equals the eye-dot's breath, so figure, loader, and status dot share one pulse.

**Spec:**

# CHARACTER & FIGURE ANIMATION GRAMMAR — Ethiopian Classical Design Language
Extends the motion spec (specs-deep-dive.md) to the illustrated figure. The figure IS the terminal's eye given a body: it behaves like ink and parchment, obeys the same governing rule (derived / decelerate-to-rest / terminate in a static resolved pose / one thing at a time), and breathes on the same cadence as the eye-dot.

## 0. Manuscript physics (governing principles)
1. The figure is a living painting. Motion derives from ink settling and the weight of umber — never from cartoon dynamics.
2. The vertical spine is sacred: frontal hieratic at all times, spine never bends in locomotion, no 3/4 turns, no profile except a full 90° procession strip (editorial only).
3. Every motion terminates in a static resolved pose. The only continuous motion is "breathing" (2400ms, ≤2px) and the ticker ribbon — the figure never loops in place, never walks on a treadmill.
4. One animated figure per viewport — the "one memorable thing" rule, same as the eye-dot.
5. Gaze is the primary animation: pupil position + blink cadence carry 80% of the character.

## 1. Base figure metrics (proportional; all motion scales linearly with figure height)
Base figure F = 180px (empty-state/editorial). Scale cascade: ticker companion 120px (×0.667), editorial hero 240–260px (×1.33–1.44), margin-note figure 96px (×0.53). At F=180: head 36px tall × 30px wide; almond eye 14×9px, pupil 4px; halo 64px disc centered behind head; shoulder width 44px; stride 24px (13%F); foot lift 10px (5.5%F); torso weight-shift ±5px (2.8%F); garment wave ±3px.

## 2. Locomotion — the icon's gait (frontal, processional)
Walk cycle 2400ms (= --dur-weave; two steps, 1200ms/step). Keyframes over the 2400ms loop:
- 0%: right foot planted; torsoX −5px; hem compressed 2px; garment wave at trough.
- 25% (600ms): right foot lifts −10px (parabolic arc, peak ~25–35%); torsoX 0; body mass leans 0.5° forward.
- 50% (1200ms): right foot plants — 120ms ease-ink settle (no overshoot); torsoX +5px; hem −2px; garment wave crest.
- 75% (1800ms): left foot lifts −10px; torsoX 0.
- 100% (2400ms): left foot plants; torsoX −5px; loop closes.
Drivers: foot lift = cubic-bezier(.45,0,.55,1) up 200ms, arc hold, ease-ink down 120ms; foot travels 12px forward per step (half stride); garment trailing edge = sine ±3px, period 2400ms, phase lag 300ms, ease-thread; torso weight transfer = ease-ink into each plant, ease-press out of it. CROWN AND HALO ARE TRANSFORM-LOCKED: translate 0, rotate 0 at every keyframe — the face never bobs. Shoulders stay level (0 rotation); hips never sway; spine vertical.
Approach choreography (the only place the walk exists — it always terminates in rest): 0ms draw-in at panel edge (§3) → 1000ms walk begins, exactly 2 steps (2400ms, max travel 48px) → 3400ms arrive → 3400–3800ms settle into the composed idle pose over 400ms ease-ink (--dur-move). If a figure must signal movement in place, it does NOT walk — the ticker ribbon flows and the figure watches; data moves, the figure does not.

## 3. The GAZE — primary character animation
Almond outline is fixed geometry (never deforms except blink lid-compression). Gaze = pupil offset within the almond: max ±5px horizontal, ±3px vertical.
- BLINK: every 4600ms ± 1400ms (seeded per figure). Close 0→90ms, lid compresses almond 9px→0, cubic-bezier(.55,0,.85,.36); hold 90–160ms closed; open 160–300ms, ease-ink. Total ~300ms. Attention (pupils locked on data) suppresses blink to 7000–9000ms; the first blink after a reaction is the released "accent blink" (90/70/140ms).
- PURSUIT: pupil travel is ease-thread (linear) with 200ms ease-ink caps at both ends. Minimum non-reaction shift 600ms (≈8px/s max). The one allowed fast move is the reaction "catch": 160ms, ease-ink, no overshoot.
- When idle, the gaze is the figure's only always-on motion — and it is never faster than a blink.

## 4. Entrance / exit — draw in like ink
ENTRANCE, total 1000ms (stroke-draw via pathLength, layered per the frame-inscription system):
- 0–140ms: halo circle stroke-draws 0→1, ease-ink (1px gold).
- 100–520ms: head + face ink outline draws top-down, 420ms, ease-ink.
- 420–880ms: torso/garment outline draws, 460ms, ease-ink, 80ms overlap with the face segment (interlace overlap, like the frame's two hands).
- 780–920ms: flat umber fields fill 0→100% opacity, 350ms, staggered 120ms, ease-ink (body before face).
- 880–1000ms: eyes — almond outlines 90ms; pupil dots pop at 940ms, 60ms ease-ink-fast cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) — the eyes arrive LAST; that is the figure "coming alive," the signature beat. 1000ms: breathing begins.
FALLBACK (raster sprite, no vector outline): opacity 0→100% + scale 0.985→1.00, 600ms ease-ink, 60ms hold; no blur, no slide, no translateY. Multiple figures stagger 150ms (max 2 per viewport).
EXIT, total 1050ms (reverse — withdraw, never vanish): 0–80ms pupils out (60ms ease-press); 60–150ms almond outlines out; 120–560ms fields lift (440ms ease-press); 420–1000ms outline draws away reversed hem→head→halo (580ms ease-press); 940–1050ms opacity 100→0 settle (110ms). Fallback: opacity 100→0 + scale 1.00→0.985, 500ms ease-press.

## 5. Looping idles
- BREATHING: 2400ms, upper chest rises 2px, ease-ink alternate — literally the eye-breath keyframe (the eye-dot breathes scale 1→1.004 over 2400ms ease-ink alternate). Figure and status dot share one pulse. Reduced to 1.5px during a stillness hold.
- GAZE SWEEP (figure at the ticker), 9.2s loop: 0–40% pupils sweep left→right (3.68s, linear with 200ms ease-ink caps); 40–48% read-hold RIGHT (the ticker side), 800ms locked; 48–88% sweep back (3.68s); 88–100% read-hold left (1.1s).
- STILLNESS HOLD: after each full loop, 1.8–2.4s where only 1.5px breathing runs, gaze locked center, no micro-motion. Pattern: move 9.2s → hold 2s → move. The painting holds; that is what makes it a living painting, not a spinning animation.
- HEAD MICRO-TILT (interest): 1.5°±0.5°, 300ms ease-ink, hold ≥1.2s, return 900ms ease-ink.

## 6. Expression timing — composure, micro-movements only
Brows = fixed ink line, never animate. Mouth = closed ink mark, never animate. Almond outline = blink only. All emotion reads through pupil position, blink cadence, and a ≤3° head tilt. Budget: ≤3 moving parts simultaneously, ≥2 frozen. State map:
- NEUTRAL/reading: blink 4.6s, pupils drift ±2px (two layered sines, periods 1.6s / 2.4s).
- INTEREST (rate catches the eye): blink suppressed 7–9s, pupils locked, head 1.5° tilt, chest breath 2px.
- ALERT (madder): one released blink at reaction end, pupils fixated (drift→0), head holds perfectly still — stillness IS the alarm.
- CALM (recovery after a move): blink 6s, drift ±3px, head returns to 0° over 900ms ease-ink.

## 7. Interaction with data — reaction to a rate tick
Total ≤3.2s, most of it stillness. Fires on the ticker swap (bounded by the ticker's own 340ms roll + 440ms wash):
- T−200ms: PRE-TICK ANTICIPATORY GAZE — pupils begin travel toward the ticker, 160ms, ease-ink. The painting listens a beat before the number changes; this is the signature behavioral beat.
- T0: number swaps. Figure: blink suppressed.
- T+260ms: head dips 1° toward the ticker, 260ms ease-ink, hold.
- T+400ms–1.4s: read — pupils locked, drift→0.
- RISE (verdigris/gold delta): T+500ms confirm — head returns to 0° over 1.1s ease-ink; optional hand-acknowledge at T+800ms (one hand rises 12px over 500ms ease-ink, holds 400ms, returns 800ms ease-ink; max once per 6s).
- FALL (madder/rubrication): T+500ms head tilts 1.5° off-axis, 300ms ease-ink; T+700ms released accent blink (90/70/140ms); T+900ms pupil flick down 2px, return 200ms; then 1.2s of stillness.
- T+1.6–3.2s: composure recovery — drift resumes, blink returns to 4.6s cadence. If the next tick arrives <3.2s later, the figure HOLDS stillness rather than resetting — it out-waits the data.
NEVER: jumping, eyes widening (geometry fixed), mouth opening, shaking, or any "surprised" reflex.

## 8. Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)
All figures render in the static composed pose: breathing 0 (a single 0.5px breath at 30% amplitude permitted only where a live panel needs one status cue), gaze center-locked, blink off, pupils centered, halo still. Entrance/exit collapse to one 250ms opacity 0→1 / 1→0, ease-ink — no stroke-draw, no scale, no slide, no stagger. Data reaction: none — state is carried by the eye-dot (gold live / red alert / pulse loading) and the delta colors; the figure is the editorial illustration, not an agent. No walk, no approach, no sweep.

## 9. Never-bounce / dignity rules (consolidated, non-negotiable)
1. Only three eases exist for figures: --ease-ink (every arrive, plant, settle, draw, fix), --ease-press (every departure, withdrawal, exit), --ease-thread linear (continuous travel: gaze pursuit, garment wave). No bounce, elastic, back, or ease-in-out arrivals.
2. Crown and halo are transform-locked in locomotion — zero vertical bob, zero rotation; the face never bobs.
3. No squash-and-stretch, no anticipation crouch, no overshoot past final keyframes.
4. No cartoon reflexes: almond geometry fixed (blink only), brows and mouth are fixed ink and never animate, no wide eyes, no jump, no shake.
5. Entrances draw in or settle; exits withdraw — never slide, pop, or scale-through-zero.
6. Every moving body has a ground anchor — a planted foot, a hem at the floor, a resting hand. Nothing floats weightless.
7. Head rotation ≤3° total, body lean ≤2°; spine vertical; no 3/4 turns.
8. One animated figure per viewport (the eye-dot's one-moment rule, extended).
9. Continuous animation is only breathing (2400ms, ≤2px) or the ticker ribbon; the figure always terminates in a static resolved pose.
10. Nothing moves faster than a blink unless it IS a blink or the 160ms pupil catch.

## 10. Rendering constraints the animation depends on (keep compatible with the figure/illustration spec)
Animate only transform + opacity + stroke-dashoffset/pathLength; never width/height/top/left. Flat fields (no gradients except parchment washes) so fields fill as opacity layers; the 1.5px ink outline lives on its own path layer for stroke-draw. Composite ≤6 layers per figure: halo / outline / body field / face field / almond pair / pupil pair — inside the ≤10 simultaneous-transform budget. The ≤30fps adaptive rule and reduced-motion collapse apply unchanged.

## 11. Timing coherence (why these numbers)
Walk cycle (2400ms) and breathing (2400ms) equal the weave loader's cycle and the eye-dot's breathe — figure, loader, and status dot share one pulse. Blinks (4.6s) and the gaze sweep (9.2s) run at 1/4–1/2 the data cadence: data changes at seconds-scale, figures move at quarter-speed. Reactions ≤3.2s bound the figure's latency to the ticker's own 340ms roll + 440ms wash + 260ms delta flap. The figure is slow, deliberate, grounded — the market's data may rush, but the gallery watches at the pace of a painting.


## Characters & illustration — the cast, its placements, and the one-figure law (roles, moments, animation hooks)

## 5. ANIMATION HOOKS — exact recipes, reusing system tokens

Discipline: characters animate ONLY in the absence of live data. A live figure is still. No blinking — breathing only. No continuous loops (no perpetual writing, pouring, or tracking): each hook is a single gesture that decelerates to rest or resolves to stillness. Motion budget: figure layers count against the ≤10 simultaneous transform/opacity animations cap; transform + opacity + stroke-dash only. prefers-reduced-motion collapses every hook to a static figure + state color.

1. **The Watcher — THE WATCH.** Eyes do not blink. They breathe (scale 1.000→1.004, 2400ms, ease-ink, alternate — the exact eye-breathe keyframe). On a data change while the Watcher is present (rare: empty→loading), the gaze makes ONE 2px saccade toward the ticker edge (60ms, ease-ink), then settles — never a glance-loop; a watch is stillness. ALERT: eyes hold red-hold #E8836F, still (ignite-then-still, per the eye-dot law).
2. **The Chronicler — THE PEN.** On article load: pen lowers (translateY 8px→0, 400ms ease-ink, ONE gesture), one ink line draws across the page (stroke-dashoffset, 700ms, inscribe ease cubic-bezier(.25,.1,.25,1)), and the line terminates in a 1px dot — the Amharic period ። — the article's kicker dot. The pen never writes continuously. This is the manuscript inscribe motion made human.
3. **The Market trader — THE POUR.** On a rate tick while the figure is present: jebena tilts 0→3° over 400ms ease-ink; one gold thread (0.9px, the weave strand) arcs spout→cup (stroke-dashoffset 700ms, ease-out); then stillness. One pour per tick (matches the 440ms delta-flash cadence, debounced the same way). The tilt is the rate micro-interaction made human; the cup fills once, then holds.

## 6. DO / DON'T — figure edition
**Do:** one figure per moment, or none; draw every figure from the Canon (§2); flat fields + 2px ink outline; key illustration chroma to story valence; hide the chrome eye-dot whenever a figure is present (one gaze per view); let a figure rest still while live data is present; reuse the motion tokens — eye-ignite, eye-breathe, inscribe ease, ease-ink, dur-settle 900 — never invent new motion; let the empty/loading/boot moments be "an empty painting of a waiting person."
**Don't:** never a mascot or logo figure (the client's rejection stands — characters illustrate, they never brand); no figure on the wordmark, tables, ticker, buttons, chips, forms, or any live instrument; never 2 figures (only 1 or 3); no flag trios on any figure or scene; no direct copies of a specific icon/face from the manuscript tradition (abstract geometry only); no photos, no gradients on figures, no shadows, no 1.5px strokes; no blinking (breath only); no continuous writing/pouring/tracking loops; no face below 32px (the motif collapses to the eye-dot); never both the figure's gaze AND the chrome eye-dot in one view.


## World & Environments — the painted stage the characters inhabit

**Verdict:** The language has characters, states, frames and chrome, but no PLACE to stand them in. Concretely missing: (1) a setting vocabulary — no defined places (street, market, ceremony interior, newsroom, manuscript plane); (2) a depth system — "no perspective" is an instruction, not a grammar; there is no layered-plane system, plane proportions, or hieratic-scale rule to replace vanishing-point space; (3) canonical object geometry — the mesob, jebena, stele, taxi, tukul, eucalyptus, awnings have no proportions or line weights, so every illustrator will re-draw them differently; (4) a symbolic light model — halos, gold fields, fire glyphs and light shafts are named but unspecified; (5) world motion — no keyframes/timing for smoke, flame, crowd, halo; (6) no reuse taxonomy mapping world motifs (jebena silhouette, mesob medallion, stele, frond, taxi stripe) to design elements, so the world can't leak into chrome with discipline. Without this, empty states and editorial heroes will each invent their own flatness.

# WORLD & ENVIRONMENTS — THE PAINTED STAGE

## 1. Principle
The world is a painted stage, not a place you walk through. Every scene is a stationary frontal tableau — a manuscript panel or wall painting that the viewer faces directly. Depth is a stack of flat cut-out planes, never a converging space. Restraint is absolute: one focal subject per scene, ≤3 figure types, ≥20% of the frame is untouched backdrop.

## 2. Canvas & unit
- Editorial hero: 1600×1000 (16:10). Square tile: 800×800 (empty / loading / chips).
- Unit U = canvas width ÷ 100 (16px on 1600, 8px on 800). Every proportion, weight and stroke below is U-relative so scenes scale across sizes.
- Line weights (stroke = ink #15090B unless noted):
  - Contour: 0.3U (4.8px @1600)
  - Structural interior line: 0.15U (2.4px)
  - Detail / fold line: 0.08U (1.3px)
  - Micro-line (weave, haräg, hatch, medallion spokes): 0.05U (0.8px)
  - Interior lines at 70% ink; pattern micro-lines in umber #8C5A2B at 40%.
- No other line weights exist. No brush variance, no opacity ramps on contours.

## 3. Depth = layered planes (perspective is banned, replaced by this)
Four planes, defined as vertical bands on the 1000px height:
- Plane A — Backdrop field (sky / gold / pattern): 0–62% (0–620px).
- Plane B — Architecture band: 62–82% (620–820px), 20% tall. Buildings, stalls, arcade, stelae, screen wall sit here.
- Plane C — Ground strip: 82–100% (820–1000px), 18% tall. Solid umber (light) or ink (dark) band = the icon's suppedaneum; figures stand on it.
- Plane D — Foreground framing edge: 0–12% from bottom (880–1000px), objects rendered at 1.4× mid-ground scale. Used ONLY to create depth via near-edge crossing; max 2 objects.
- Every subject faces the viewer. The camera never moves, never tilts, never zooms; there is no camera at all.

## 4. Composition grammar
- Hieratic scale (size = importance, NOT distance): main subject 55–65% of frame height; secondary 35–45%; architecture 20–30%. No size-shrink for depth.
- Axis rule: single-subject scenes center on the X=50% vertical axis; the halo centers on the same axis.
- Frieze rule: multiple figures all stand feet-on-groundline, same scale, equal importance; overlap is the only depth cue (rear figures rise above the front ground strip, never shrink).
- Negative space: ≥20% backdrop untouched; on patterned backdrops keep a 15% clear zone around the focal subject.
- Focal gold field (optional): a flat gold panel 1.4× the focal subject's bounding box sits behind it in Plane A.
- Frame variant: when in-frame, the haräg interlace border occupies the outer 4% of all edges with rivet lozenges at corners (established system); content insets 4%.

## 5. Light — symbolic, never physical
- Halo: 3 flat annulus rings behind the ONE primary figure — outer gold #C9962E, mid saffron #E8A33D, inner parchment-gold #F5E9D1; radii R / 0.72R / 0.5R with ring gaps 0.04R; optional ink contour on the outer ring. Max one halo per scene.
- Gold field wash: Plane A may be Field gold #D9B45C, rendered as a HARD 2-step banded wash — top band #E6C97E, bottom band #B8842E. This is the ONLY gradient-like treatment in the entire language, and it is a two-flat-band poster split, never a smooth gradient.
- Contact ground (optional): solid groundline shadow — a 0.5U-tall umber band directly under large objects (taxi, mesob, buildings); OFF for small objects. No cast or directional shadow ever.
- Fire glyph (brazier): 3 flat tongues — central madder #A62F1E triangle, 2 flanking saffron #E8A33D triangles, thin ink contour, NO glow, NO bloom.
- Window light shaft: at most ONE flat gold #C9962E parallelogram from a madder-framed window slit, down across Plane B onto the ground strip; no blend, hard edges.

## 6. World pigments (additions to the established palette)
- Umber #8C5A2B (earth / wood), deep umber #5C3317 (shadow wood), burnt umber #6B3015 (soil / roofs).
- Field gold #D9B45C; old-gold wash bands #E6C97E (top) / #B8842E (bottom).

### Character animation production spec — 12fps hold grid, keyframe tables, pixel-snap/fill-registration, handcraft offset, light↔dark swap

ANIMATION PRODUCTION SPEC

GRID. Author every loop on a 12fps integer frame grid (1 frame = 1/12s); all timing quantized to whole frames. Keyframes land on whole frames only. Easing is linear throughout — no bounce, overshoot, or ease-elastic. Hold discipline: each loop ends on a frame identical to F0 and rests there ≥2 frames before repeating. Motion lands, then holds; hieratic stillness is the default state.

PIXEL-SNAP & FILL-REGISTRATION. All geometry sits on integer px. Silhouette (2px at canon, 3px @192) and structural strokes (eye outline, nose, mouth) snap to whole px; curves snap to the 1px grid with zero anti-aliasing fuzz. Every color region under-draws its outline by exactly 1px (fill edge = outline centerline inset 1px on all sides); verify no ground-colored hairline at any fill/stroke seam.

HANDCRAFT OFFSET. To keep flat fields from reading as clip-art, interior detail strokes — shamma fold-lines and hem stripes only — are deliberately NOT grid-snapped: each centerline sits 0.5px off the integer grid, offset direction alternating +0.5/−0.5px per stroke. Fold-lines are non-uniform: never parallel, never evenly spaced; length varies 1–3px, spacing varies 1px. The offset set is fixed per figure (recorded once), theme-independent, and frozen during every loop (offsets do not animate — else they flicker). The silhouette never jitters; only interior line-work carries the offset.

KEYFRAME TABLES (all frames whole, all linear):

1. DRIFT (idle; 19-frame loop = 1.583s; canon 1.6s quantized). Lateral sway ±1px + rotation ±1°, halo rigid. F0 x0/rot0 (hold F0–F3) · F4 x+1/rot+1° · F9 x0/rot0 · F14 x−1/rot−1° · F18 x0/rot0 → loops to F0.

### Pre-tick anticipatory gaze (T−200ms)

PRE-TICK ANTICIPATORY GAZE — the signature behavioral beat, deepened.

1. PUPIL-TRAJECTORY MATH (overshoot-free fix). Anticipation is a single pupil-pair glide, start→lock, always 160ms, ease-ink — the one allowed fast move. Overshoot-free contract, four parts. (a) Known start: drift is stopped first — drift→0 in 160ms ease-ink beginning T−400ms, so by T−240ms pupils are fully stationary and the glide starts from a resolvable coordinate, never a mid-sine value. (b) Capped curve: use ease-ink as cubic-bezier(.42,0,.58,1) with both control-point y ≤ 1.0 — a control rising past 1.0 is the sole overshoot source, so it is prohibited. (c) Tangent landing: the terminal 10% (16ms) of the glide is remapped to ease-thread (linear) so the pupil decelerates INTO lock and can never cross it. (d) Hard clamps: lock = ticker-side read-lock (±5px horizontal, −1px vertical bias on a 4px pupil inside the 14×9 almond; max ±5×±3); every sample ≤ ±5px. Path is a straight chord — no arc, no crouch (dignity rule 3). During the 160ms glide nothing else may move; the halo, head, and brows are frozen.

2. PER-CADENCE LATENCY TUNING. τ = ticker roll (340ms standard / 460ms slow); wash scales 440→590ms, delta flap 260→350ms. Lead L = τ × 0.6, clamped [180, 300]ms → 200ms @340, 276ms @460. Glide fires at T−L, arrives T−L+160ms (T−40ms @340; T−116ms @460) → a pre-swap micro-lock of 40–120ms that grows with cadence: a slower roll reads as a more patient hold. Anticipation keys off the swap event T0 ONLY — never the wash or ribbon scroll (the figure ignores the ribbon's own motion). If the next tick arrives < 3.2s later: no re-anticipation; figure holds stillness and out-waits the data.

3. RISE BRANCH (verdigris/gold delta). T0 swap, blink suppressed. T+260ms head dips 1° toward ticker, 260ms ease-ink, hold. T+400ms–1.4s read-lock, drift→0. T+500ms confirm: head returns to 0° over 1.1s ease-ink. HAND-ACKNOWLEDGE VARIANT (max once per 6s; offered only when the rise delta exceeds 1 full point): at T+800ms the far-side hand lifts 12px over 500ms ease-ink, thumb-arc only, palm never rotates; holds 400ms perfectly still — exactly 2 moving parts (hand + chest breath ≤2px), 4 frozen (gaze locked, head 0°, brows, mouth, dress hem); returns T+1.7s over 800ms ease-ink. No other body part may move during the hand.

4. FALL BRANCH (madder/rubrication delta). T+500ms head tilts 1.5° off-axis, 300ms ease-ink. T+700ms released accent blink (90/70/140ms). T+900ms pupil flick down 2px, return 200ms ease-ink. T+1.2s stillness. No hand in the fall branch. Both branches terminate in a static resolved pose by T+3.2s.

5. DO / DON'T. Do: freeze drift before the glide; animate transform only (pupil pair on its own layer, ≤6-layer budget); respect the three-ease rule. Don't: overshoot, arc the path, widen pupils (geometry fixed), "anticipate the anticipation," react to the wash, or move the head during the glide.

S3 — BUNA INTERIOR, DEDICATED PASS. Canvas 1600×1000; U=16. The frame is perfectly axis-centered on X=50% (x800); the ceremony IS the painting. The single window slit + light shaft is the sole sanctioned asymmetry.

PLANES. A backdrop 0–70% (y0–700): parchment #FCF9F3 or the old-gold 2-band wash (#E6C97E top / #B8842E bottom, hard poster split, never a gradient). B arcade block 53–75%. C floor 75–100% (y750–1000) solid umber #8C5A2B; scattered verdigris grass-dashes (0.08U strokes, ~14, y780–980, none within 12U of the axis below the brazier). Plane D unused — the rečot frieze is mid-ground on Plane C (canonical scale, no 1.4×).

ARCADE (3 round arches, church derivation). Total width 51U (816px) centered, x392–1208; four parchment columns w3U; three equal openings 13U. Column: base 0.8U on y750, shaft 6.5U (to y646) with slight entasis (taper to 2.6U); capital = flat madder #A62F1E band 1.2U (y646–634) flaring to a 3.4U abacus + 0.3U gold fillet beneath — an echo of the jebena's madder/gold equator bands. Arches spring y634, semicircular r6.5U, keystone apex y530; all three identical (frieze). Nothing stands in the openings (restraint).

CEREMONY ENSEMBLE (axis; modest pot, monumental light). Baseline y880. Front-to-back z-order: incense pan, brazier, jebena, rečot frieze frontmost. Incense pan: small m'eqad H2U W6U at x570–630 (y860–880), one ink charcoal disc, its own 3-tongue fire glyph at 0.8×. Brazier: m'eqad H3U W10U dead-center, x752–848, y848–880, two ink charcoal discs, fire glyph on top. Jebena at 6U total height (canonical proportions scaled: base ellipse y838, bulb center y792, neck to y758, lip y752), seated on the brazier, spout to upper-right. Rečot frieze: 3 cups, baseline y900, centers x800 and 800±12U, H1.3U (canonical vs jebena), gold rim band, one madder dot facing viewer.

HALO + SHAFT (the two light symbols). Halo in Plane A, center (800,470): outer ring R230 gold #C9962E, mid 0.72R=166 saffron #E8A33D, inner 0.5R=115 parchment-gold #F5E9D1, gaps 0.04R≈9; optional 0.3U ink contour on the outer ring. One halo behind the whole ceremony; arcade columns cross in front (stacked planes, no blend). Window slit: madder-framed, 2.5U×8U, at x24% (y480–608). Light shaft: ONE flat gold #C9962E parallelogram, hard edges, no blend; from the slit's lower edge at 55° down to Plane C, 5U→9U wide, y608–940, landing ≈x616. It crosses the incense pan and stops short of the rečot line — cups stay unlit parchment.

MOTION (the sanctioned pair, ≤2 animated elements). Incense spiral: two warm-charcoal #4A3A33 strands (0.08U), rising 15U from the pan; loop 12s ease-in-out; phase offset 4s; alpha 100/55/0 in three steps, never smooth. Flame flip (brazier): 2-frame cut-out alternation, tongue A up / tongue B up, 2s cycle; 3 flat tongues — central madder #A62F1E triangle, 2 flanking saffron #E8A33D — thin ink contour, NO glow, NO bloom.

DO/DON'T. Do: hold the axis; ≥20% untouched backdrop; let halo + shaft carry all emotion; echo madder/gold band motifs between capital, jebena, cups. Don't: any figure (this is an object painting); candles or lamps (fire is the single source, symbolized); cast shadow or floor reflection; faces or the cross as brand; a second window or shaft; any smooth tween.
