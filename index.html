Privacy & parents

Everything runs on your device: your photo or video is never uploaded, and this page makes no server requests. Controller: the site operator. Your device stores only your language, theme, and letter choices plus the app's own files - no personal data is uploaded or stored on our servers. Optional analytics are off by default and only send anonymous event counts if the site owner enables them. For parents: no accounts, no cookies, no tracking - a child's photo never leaves their device. Please ask before sharing a real person's picture.
